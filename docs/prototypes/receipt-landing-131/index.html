    My recommendation for discussion

    Keep squash and develop explicit input correspondence, with retained evaluated source and processing provenance, the original comparison, and an independent check of the actual landed commit. A fresh consumer needs a fetchable retained reference or source archive; a digest alone cannot recover missing history.

    An ancestry-preserving merge is the simpler alternative for the Receipt adapter. It still needs the final-commit consumer and would require changing the currently allowed merge operations. Fast-forward only fails when the target diverges; ordinary rebasing does not guarantee the evaluated commit survives.

    The prototype question: Is the extra correspondence and evidence-retention machinery an acceptable direction to take into the separate strategy decision, or would you rather pursue ancestry-preserving landing?
