# Comparing concrete receiver-observation approaches

This packet supplies the three source-design handoffs for
[Compare and review the parallel observation candidates](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/387).
The join consumes the final reports, this method, the source correction, and
the evidence manifest at one published revision. Component selection remains
with [Choose observation components from the parallel comparison](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/388).

## Fixed increment

The accepted increment develops three competing designs, with targeted
source/synthetic prototypes only when they resolve a meaningful uncertainty.
It preserves the full notification and before/after actual-state observation
contract in the [accepted parallel decision](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/359#issuecomment-5957424708).
The lanes compare existing observations, receiver hooks, and a minimal app-side
observer. The last lane distinguishes temporary diagnosis from an ongoing
dependency. Fixture acquisition compares bounded identity discovery with the
previously designed app-side receipt.

No live Desktop access, installation, hook activation, debugger attachment,
private receiver read, task creation, prompt, send, or restart is part of this
increment. The work does not adopt a component or revise the qualification bar.

## Independence and exchange

Three fresh leader-owned native children received the same
[published seed](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/blob/6fbddb3883e08476af9fffe1410863f0e89f0cd6/docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-01-observer-scope-review.md),
their separate lane charters, named offline source, and permission to consult
public primary sources. Initial independence concerns their new findings;
the shared prior research was explicit. Each returned its report before
receiving either sibling's new work.

The coordinator captured each terminal Markdown response exactly from its
identified native child, corroborated by the live result, then hashed all three
before exchange. The coordinator independently checked the
[task-creation source correction](source-correction.md). The exchange bound
those three reports and that check. Each lane then received the same frozen
exchange for cross-examination and a focused continuation.

The [evidence manifest](evidence.json) separates captured hashes from published
hashes. Publication substitutes semantic input names for local paths and
normalizes the final newline. First-round factual content remains unchanged;
the final reports and source correction control the current interpretation.
Input/exposure inventories are retained in the reports.

Workers were requested as GPT-6.1 Sol xhigh through the native collaboration
tools. The coordinator retained separate model-selection records and frozen
invocation plans. This records same-leader observations and requested selection,
without authenticated model-execution or product-enforced authority claims.
The work used `research`, `choosing-agent-models`, and
`delegating-cross-agent-work`. Independent final review follows `tricritical`.

## Prototype and evidence discipline

A prototype earns its place by answering a source/design question. Inventing
an event or parser fixture cannot establish that the selected running receiver
produces it. Each lane must justify reuse or identify a concrete remaining
source experiment and its test seam. Historical test results remain attributed
to their retained reports unless rerun; this packet does not claim a new test
pass merely because a prior prototype is useful.

Producer identity, access path, evidence meaning, and freshness are separate
questions. An internal getter is not an external endpoint; cached, saved,
displayed, and event-time state do not automatically describe current applied
state. A directory-entry projection and a whole-file read with filtered output
also have different exposure. Preserve these distinctions in the selection
and subsequent procedure.
