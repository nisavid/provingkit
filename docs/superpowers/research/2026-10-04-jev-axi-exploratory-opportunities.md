# Explore useful roles before qualifying assignments

The catalog reassessment includes discovering better implementations and new
purposes for `jev-axi` hooks and their shared components. Its next research
stage considers those possibilities before selecting more comparison
contracts. Jev remains one possible implementation of a bounded judgment,
alongside ordinary agent reasoning, deterministic methods, and omission.

The operator requested this broader exploration after the
[limited Stop audit](evidence/jev-axi-retained-stop-audit-2026-10-04/README.md).
That audit found no demonstrated missed current requirement or separately
removable review step in eight surviving boundaries. Missing full records
limit its findings. It did not evaluate alternative purposes or establish
that completion assistance has no value.

The earlier mandatory stop-and-ask correction concerned **PostToolUse
supervision**, not Stop. Its bounded evidence and the earlier supervision,
authorization, and commit-message no-advance decisions remain valid within
their tested scope. Exploration does not reinstate a removed instruction or
justify a retained assessment.

## Discovery and qualification answer different questions

A research hypothesis needs a plausible purpose, a beneficiary, an intelligible
mechanism, and an observation that could change our view. It can originate in
an incident, a successful but costly workflow, first-principles reasoning, or
external work. It need not wait for a failure in a convenient retained sample.

Qualification keeps the accepted reliability and whole-workflow requirements.
An agent-facing addition must show a useful effect in native tasks; a
replacement must remove real work while preserving quality. The operator also
accepted measured reduction of their verification or resumption effort as a
possible primary benefit, with a separate acceptance bar: trustworthy evidence,
demonstrably less effort, no extra unnecessary questions, and no loss of task
quality. A plausible score or delivered note establishes none of these results.
New purposes require their own accepted contrasts and critical cases. Research
cost is recorded separately from recurring operating cost; it still matters
to choosing the next investigation.

Separate five choices: the **purpose**, its **consumer**, the **event or
on-demand entry point**, the **implementation**, and its **authority**. For
example, useful completion evidence might be collected deterministically,
checked with a narrow Jev question, reviewed by an agent, or used only in a
handoff. None of those choices entails blocking a Stop event.

## Organize opportunities by need

The cross-informed [Claude critique](evidence/jev-axi-exploratory-opportunities-2026-10-04/claude-critique.md)
identified a bias in a catalog with an opportunity under every hook: coverage
can look like a requirement to give every event a job. The investigation
therefore follows needs: completion assurance, continuity, discovery, review
allocation, and repair of recurring environment problems. The family table
below checks coverage; it is not a list of assignments to fill. A need may be
served entirely by the ordinary harness, and a hook may receive no job.

The critique also exposed hidden producers. Selecting a useful next debugging
observation, writing a review question, composing a handoff, and generating a
repair suggestion all need an agent or another implementation. Jev could
classify supplied alternatives or evidence; their generation is paid work.
The source and cost of current requirements and amendments must be established
before relying on them. This does not preselect a new shared requirements
ledger: existing native records and bounded retrieval remain alternatives.

## Stop deserves several distinct investigations

| Hypothesis | Proposed benefit and consumer | First discriminating work | What could defeat it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interpret the boundary before checking completeness | The active agent avoids irrelevant assessment when yielding, handing off, or waiting | Compare explicit native state and ordinary response interpretation; identify the ambiguity left for a typed judgment | Collecting state costs more than the avoided work, or legitimate pauses are misread |
| Match completion claims to applicable evidence | The completing agent or reviewer catches an unsupported claim or avoids repeating an actual evidence-reconciliation step | Map current requirements, claims, candidate identity, and verification in a bounded example; compare deterministic presence/freshness checks with narrow semantic relevance | The source evidence is unavailable or merely self-reported; constructing the map duplicates the review |
| Route unresolved questions to focused review | A coordinator spends expensive review capacity where an unresolved question requires it | Locate an actual optional review-allocation decision, then compare explicit rules, ordinary planning, and typed routing | Routing costs as much as review, misses a necessary review, or only renames work already performed |
| Preserve useful state at a pause or handoff | The resuming agent avoids rediscovery or repeated attempts | Inspect one handoff/resumption pair, locate actual rediscovery, and compare a minimal deterministic record with semantic selection | The record is unused, stale, incomplete, or duplicates ordinary handoff work |

Required reviews remain required. A routing hypothesis does not authorize
skipping them. A Jev judgment can select or assess supplied candidates; it
cannot generate a reliable handoff, recover absent evidence, or operate an
application to verify its behavior. Those producer and consumer costs belong
to the proposed workflow.

The missing original experiment records are also a concrete continuity
problem in this effort. Durable retention is primarily a storage and lifecycle
question; it is not, by itself, a reason to assign another judgment to Jev.

The strongest initial leads are claim-to-evidence reconciliation, assessment
timing informed by boundary meaning and changed evidence, and continuity at a
handoff. This is a research priority proposal, not an assignment. The operator
accepted investigating alternative delivery points tied to these catalog
needs: task completion, requirement amendments, compaction or explicit handoff,
and on-demand use. That choice does not expand this effort into an unrelated
agent platform. Operator-facing verification or resumption benefits are also
eligible under the separate bar above; each candidate still needs concrete
cases and an observation contract.

## Apply the same search across the catalog

| Family | Alternative or improved-purpose hypothesis | Simpler comparison and principal uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SessionStart | Deliver a small task-relevant capability map; retrieve readiness and detailed guidance at use time | Native discovery, deterministic lookup, and omission; startup may precede enough task context to select anything |
| PreToolUse | Surface an ordinary action/target discrepancy early, with adequate current intent, instead of treating hazard or location as intent | Argument/target checks and ordinary previews; interpretation and security efficacy need their separate qualified research |
| PostToolUse | Detect when another attempt adds no information; route to a different available observation or a maintainer's later environment repair | Repeated-error rules, ordinary debugging, and omission; repetition may be legitimate and history preparation expensive |
| pre-commit | Focus an existing staged-change review on a specific unresolved contract question | Linters, tests, path-based review selection, and ordinary review; partial staging or absent callers may defeat the semantic question |
| commit-msg | Help reconstruct the committed scope before message authoring, or test a narrow material claim against the staged change | Ordinary authoring and templates; the agent may already know the answer, and the commit-msg event may be too late |
| pre-push | Reconcile operational obligations over the actual pushed range, reusing earlier dispositions | CI, release manifests, and ordinary review; a warning may duplicate earlier work or refer to the wrong range |

For commit preparation, a generated outline is an integration proposal, not a
Jev output capability. A possible typed subtask is selecting which supplied
claims are supported by the staged change. Candidate generation and final
message writing remain costs, and may make the whole proposal unattractive.

Shared context selection, assessment cadence, cache invalidation, batching,
abstention, diagnostics, and accounting receive the same treatment. Name the
consumer and its decision before collecting another score. Prefer explicit
state and dependency rules where they suffice; investigate a semantic
judgment only for the ambiguity that remains. Count shared work once and
retain the cost of constructing and refreshing it.

## Research method and joins

Use parallel conceptual, primary-source, and incident/source lanes for a
bounded question. Preserve their first outputs and input exposure before
cross-informed critique. Reconcile contrary evidence and missing premises;
agreement between researchers is not behavioral validation.

Each candidate records its purpose, consumer, proposed output, required
context and producer, possible typed judgment, alternatives, predicted benefit
or work removed, legitimate and adverse contrasts, defeat condition, and
cheapest informative next observation. Successful tasks can expose avoidable
work; synthetic examples can test feasibility. Neither establishes native
added value by itself.

Review representative available records rather than selecting only a striking
case. Record the selection rule, missing records, and ordinary unaided
successes; where feasible, label the decision-time evidence before revealing
the outcome. Counts in a selected or incomplete corpus are descriptive, not
failure rates or the maximum addressable share of a classifier. Test the
incremental value of Jev separately from improvements to the integration.

Account for synchronous latency, attention displaced by notes, context
preparation, maintenance, and rework from false discrepancies. Prompt-cache
effects remain a measurement question, not an assumed saving or penalty.
Choose the next investigation for the decision it can change rather than
launching one experiment for every idea in the portfolio.

At the join, distinguish: ready for a bounded observation proposal; promising
but missing a named premise; unsupported for a stated reason; or outside the
catalog with a named follow-up owner. Continue when a material contradiction
or promising unexamined mechanism remains. A fixed number of ideas, sources,
or rounds does not establish sufficient exploration. Conversely, do not keep
searching indefinitely to justify an existing hook.

The maintained `handling-sys1-incidents` comparison contract remains the
qualification consumer, with `adopting-jev` supplying judgment-fit guidance.
This report captures the exploratory handoff for this catalog; it does not
expand the incident intake aid into a general research procedure or install
an unsettled convention in shared equipment.
