## What the measurements support

The two request phases are separate invocations. Their reported values are historical API-rate equivalents, not invoices, subscription quota, active effort, or complete workflow costs. [Review usage](review-usage.json) records the separate research overhead: cumulative native-client input, cached input, output, reasoning, approximate session spans, and requested-model API-rate equivalents. Provider attestation and complete coordinator attribution remain unavailable. The final review's own counters were acquired after it completed and are not part of its reviewed inputs.

The [claim contract](../jev-axi-comparison-runners-2026-10-07/claim-execution-contract.md), [coverage contract](../jev-axi-comparison-runners-2026-10-07/coverage-execution-contract.md), and [whole-invocation accounting contract](../jev-axi-comparison-runners-2026-10-07/invocation-accounting.md) govern this evidence. Original inputs, provider records, fixed evaluator keys, answers, and grades remain retained locally. Public projections omit local paths and do not substitute for those full records.
