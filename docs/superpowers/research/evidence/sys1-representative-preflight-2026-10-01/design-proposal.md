# Prepare one representative qualification batch

The next useful decision is whether to broaden the workload to ordinary developer tasks that naturally require more work. I recommend that change, followed by at most one passive, multi-turn episode per harness on the same task. This is a proposal for a separately accepted execution contract. It does not authorize native runs or select a supervision arm.

## The operator decision

The small repair and report tasks often finish before cadence ten. Keeping them is valid, but repeated attempts would add little unless the task or qualification question changes.

Choose between:

- **Broaden the workload, recommended:** prepare a modest, realistic change spanning existing implementation, tests, and documentation, with genuine same-session requirement amendments. This can provide natural cadence opportunities while preserving ordinary instructions and successful agent behavior as valid outcomes. Results would apply to this broader workload, not automatically to the original small tasks.
- **Keep the current workload:** retain the observed lack of opportunities, stop further unchanged preflight attempts, and leave unsupported comparison claims unresolved. Omission remains eligible; sparse opportunities do not establish a universal assignment.

This choice changes the population being studied and needs the operator's decision. Cadence ten, the four-question batch, precedence, and common Stop treatment in the formal comparison remain fixed. Lower cadence, compulsory mistakes, and extra calls solely to reach ten are outside this proposal.

Reuse the retained controller correction and its eight local checks and replay. ## What the bounded batch must establish

Freeze at most two serialized passive sessions, one per harness, with explicit deadlines and retention limits, no automatic retries, and independent source/context and accounting reviews on the final preparation revision. Retain the original launch checks, attempt accounting, and failure distinctions. Changed versions or unsupported selections require adjudication before execution.

For Codex, require complete ordered continuation through the final terminal receipt, acceptance of cross-turn reused hook IDs, and usable shell scratch during native work. Retain the incomplete prior episode as a failure; the patch cannot repair its missing terminal evidence.

At actual cadence-ten boundaries, retain the contemporaneous assessment input and available transcript, tool results, files, counts, and baseline. Determine whether an intermediate amendment is absent from the projection and whether other available context supplies it. Stop-boundary omission cannot qualify a PostToolUse contrast. Failure-event coverage remains per-property; neither failed commands nor loops must occur for mechanical lifecycle qualification.

## Admission and stopping conditions

A completed lifecycle qualifies only the observed transport and profile. A cadence opportunity qualifies the observed assessment-input shape. Neither establishes wrong judgments, useful steering, avoided interruptions, or saved expensive-model work.

Preserve all naturally observed outcomes. Legitimate repeated verification and an explicitly authorized format reversal remain required comparison cases; a failure-oriented workload cannot substitute for them. An obsolete requirement or loop counts only when the agent actually exhibits it. The deterministic comparator remains conditional on its supported native predicate and meaningful opportunity. Diagnostic scores require a named consumer and demonstrated benefit.

If the bounded batch still produces no relevant cadence boundary, stop qualifying that property. If cadence occurs but the critical behavior does not, retain successful task completion and leave usefulness unestablished. Do not enlarge or rerun the task automatically. Report the supported subset and the unresolved decision: accept a narrower study or separately choose another ordinary workload. Keep the full comparison unadmitted where its required cases lack evidence.

Reliability and legitimate-work quality remain primary; interruptions and constrained capacity follow. Record money and latency separately, retain failed preparation costs, and leave unmeasured quantities unknown.
