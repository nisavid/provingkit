# Prepare an observation of early summary-checking inputs

This preparation defines how to observe whether a saved summary and its explicitly linked report can become usable inputs while checking still matters. It joins independent workload and accounting investigations into an [observation protocol](contract.md). A current authoring event is not yet bound, so the protocol is not ready for execution acceptance.

The proposed System One judgment is whether a summary faithfully represents its identified report. For example, a summary could say an approach is ready to use while its report recommends further observation. Code can preserve the draft, report, link, and surrounding qualifications; a later semantic comparison would test the remaining judgment. This preparation runs no assessment and establishes no model benefit.

## What the investigations established

The [workload investigation](workload-first-pass.md) identifies the needed event: a naturally saved draft with an unchanged material attribution to a complete linked report, before a specifically named review or reuse decision. The [accounting investigation](accounting-first-pass.md) keeps actual ordinary work separate from all additional selection, copying, preparation, inspection, and observation effort. The raw reports retain their original repository-root-relative references.

Both investigators used the same sixteen frozen inputs, returned independently before synthesis, and completed with their current-workload gap explicit. Their input and result identities are retained in [the preparation record](preparation-record.json). The coordinator verified the inputs against the published source revision and closed both workers and their controllers. This is ordinary same-leader execution evidence; it does not attest the effective model or enforce read-only authority.

The [qualified snapshot producer](../jev-axi-comparison-producers-2026-10-07/README.md#saved-draft-input) removes the requirement to commit a draft before packaging it. It does not identify a useful authoring event, establish sufficient context, or deliver anything to a consumer. Those remain distinct observation questions.

## Joined protocol

The protocol preserves one initial draft/report pair, selects the first material attribution in a bound section, and permits one invocation of the pinned producer. Later ordinary amendments remain separate records and do not trigger another invocation. Missing inputs, an elapsed opportunity, exhausted limits, or a failed attempt remain outcomes without retries or replacement workloads.

The proposed budget includes derived output, repeated reads, complete invocation time, ordinary work, observer effort, failed or unused work, and explicit measurement gaps. Its sixty-minute whole-attempt window includes acquisition, reconciliation, independent result review, and handback. These are proposed experiment limits, not accepted execution authority or general reliability thresholds.

The observer-created package stays with the observer. The study can establish timely input availability for the tested coordinator-mediated route; it cannot establish actual consumer delivery, useful correction, or savings. A slow manual route also cannot decide whether a future automatic delivery mechanism would work.
