# Transfer judgment-design methods without transferring performance claims

System One and the TypeSafe image-model effort exchanged their existing
findings and planned methods. The image-model work is now represented by
[Fathomwork's accepted visual-evaluation trial](https://github.com/nisavid/fathomwork/issues/121#issuecomment-6283769197)
and [prototype](https://github.com/nisavid/fathomwork/issues/122).
This exchange changed neither experiment.

## Fathomwork to System One

The owner reports a bounded visible-appearance question: whether the host/base
slab reads as credible stone, contradicts stone, or lacks enough visible
evidence. It does not ask the model to determine physical composition. The
consumer decides whether a reusable advisory screening procedure deserves
independent validation; the procedure cannot accept a specimen or replace final
human judgment.

The four related photographs are development material, with human reference
labels supplied before trial answers. Fourteen initial tactics imply 54
applicable requests; two negative-reference cases have no eligible reference.
There is no validation set. The initial sweep precedes refinement, and a
contender must be one reusable procedure rather than a hindsight combination
of the best answer for each image. Fresh independent cases are required for
claims about new images.

The planned baseline comprises four isolated native Sol 6.1 High reviews, one
per photograph, using the material guidance, permitted references, normal
inspection, and subsequent human judgment. No inference or baseline review
had executed at this exchange.

The applicable lesson is to name the consumer's observable target before
constructing a judgment. System One's compatibility task already has such a
boundary: existing consumers should keep their behavior after an opt-in format
is added. The image design does not prove that text interpretation is cheap,
that a semantic check adds value, or that either domain's results generalize.

## System One to Fathomwork

The [direct-versus-decomposed result](../jev-axi-decomposed-judgment-results-2026-10-15/results.md)
retains the concrete ambiguity failures. Jev 1.14 treated “the screening” and
“the new pear” as settled referents despite distinct plausible bindings.
All 13 pairs with usable judgments on both sides retained their direct result:
eleven correct and two incorrect. No wrong-to-right transition was observed.
Some ordinary-model component disagreements were hidden by earlier combination
gates, which is why intermediate answers remain diagnostically useful.

The [real-input investigation](../jev-axi-real-input-feasibility-2026-10-19/README.md)
found that source identities do not enumerate every valid interpretation.
Supplying irrelevant alternatives or an unjustified Cartesian product can
create false ambiguity; silently missing an interpretation is an input failure.
Complete interpretation-production effort remains unmeasured. Source-derived
operation counts are not a measurement of recurring workflow cost.

The [probability-contract investigation](../jev-probability-contract-2026-10-17/README.md)
found a distribution totaling 0.99 and no verified provider guarantee for the
comparison's very tight local tolerance. Confidence is distinct from selected
probability, and neither field establishes calibration. Preserve raw values and
interpret them under the frozen consumer contract; do not change prior grades
after seeing an inconvenient result.

Finally, [delivery preparation](../jev-axi-summary-delivery-preparation-2026-10-20/contract.md)
separates available inputs from adequate context, delivery, consumption, useful
correction, and savings. In the earlier observation, draft capture finished
19.378 seconds before the owner's reported review-launch acknowledgment, but
selection/acquisition finished 40.762 seconds afterward. That acknowledgment
does not establish actual review start. A correct answer on supplied input
therefore would not establish that a check arrived early enough to help.

The Fathomwork owner identified these concrete ambiguity categories, matched
comparisons, preparation burdens, and downstream-effect evidence as useful for
interpreting its later results. Public pointers were sent; no images, private
labels, request bodies, or raw experiment logs were exchanged.

## Disposition of the shared lessons

| Lesson | Present use | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fix the observable target and consumer decision | Clarify the compatibility baseline and interpret image development results. | No new behavior assignment. |
| Keep development and fresh validation separate | Preserve constructed text cases and image tactic development as their actual evidence class. | Small related sets provide no population error rate. |
| Preserve component answers, referrals, and missing inputs | Diagnose why a combined procedure answers correctly or incorrectly. | More components are not presumed to improve quality or economy. |
| Compare complete workflows | Include preparation, human labels, failures, remaining review, maintenance, and unnecessary interruption. | Unknown costs stay unknown; API-equivalent value is not native billing. |
| Preserve experiment contracts | Use new lessons to motivate later proposals. | No retrospective regrading, trial expansion, or live integration. |

These methods need no new shared framework. The existing research, incident,
and comparison procedures remain the maintained consumer paths. Any reusable
equipment change needs evidence of a distinct missing behavior and its own
owner; this exchange does not install an unsettled convention.
