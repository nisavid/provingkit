## Choices made at the join

The proposals agree on prospective selection, the distinction between replacing
reasoning and adding independent checking, and the limits of a passive episode.
I selected the accounting proposal's smaller envelope: thirty minutes of
collection, three report checkpoints, two sources at most, ten artifact captures,
8 MiB of gross reads, and 2 MiB of retained workload content. A further ten
minutes closes the records without acquiring more workload content. A partial
result remains useful if it states which question it cannot answer.

I excluded both optional producer invocations. The existing claim producer needs
committed inputs, while the natural early draft is a working file. This
observation can record that timing mismatch without making the author create
another commit or adding new equipment. No model hint is delivered to the author.

The workload owner confirmed that its ordinary workflow saves and hashes the
returned first draft before independent review and retains review records tied
to that draft. This supplies a natural checkpoint. The report remained unwritten
and unreviewed at that confirmation. Fresh admission still matters: if this
window passes before acceptance and collection, the contract records the missed
opportunity instead of substituting a later draft.

The reports differ slightly about eligibility. I chose the stricter prospective
first-draft boundary, preserving clear selection order. It does not imply that
an author who already formed a judgment cannot benefit from independent checking.
The observer's acquisition delay and missing earlier work remain explicit.
