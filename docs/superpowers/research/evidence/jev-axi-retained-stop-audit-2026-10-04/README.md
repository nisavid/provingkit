# Retained Stop evidence audit

The surviving evidence from eight Stop boundaries does not establish a missed current requirement or a distinct completion-review step that a Jev assessment could replace. This is an incomplete re-audit: the original experiment directories are no longer available. It supports no Stop assessment advancement, general utility conclusion, or economic ranking.

The accepted focus was to inspect retained Stop evidence before considering more experiments. It belongs to [Choose the SessionStart and Stop observation scopes](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/421), under the [accepted family purposes and bars](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/339#issuecomment-5961665316). Stop's purpose is to catch unmet current requirements when an agent claims completion, while respecting pauses, bounded handoffs, and amendments. The next scope decision remains open.

## Evidence available now

The six small-task boundaries and two representative Codex boundaries were captured during earlier passive episodes. No Jev assessment or steering occurred at those boundaries. This audit ran no new experimental episode, test, hook, or Jev request. Research workers and recovery effort are additional research costs.

At resumption, 88 selected original files from the ordinary cohort and 79 from the representative cohort were missing. The two experiment roots had been kept under temporary storage; their current absence is observed, but its cause is not established. All 34 surviving selected inputs matched their frozen identities. Before the pause, two mutable representative project files had already differed from their historical hashes and were excluded.

Pre-pause native tool outputs preserved enough text to recover 22 JSON files with their original SHA-256 values: six manifests, eight completed-turn agreement records, two process records, four delivery records, and two outcome records. Selected fields from all eight Stop records also survive, including completion reports and constructed observations. Those field projections have separate provenance; no complete original hook record was recovered. The selected original native stdout logs and final project files remain unavailable.
