# Comparison Contract

Use this when an experiment could change the selected correction or decide
whether a behavior belongs with Sys1. Compare complete workflows at acceptable
reliability. Recovery after an intervention is an outcome to account for, not
by itself a reason to retain that intervention. Use `adopting-jev` for Jev fit,
question design, and integration choices; this contract owns the incident
comparison and its handback.

Freeze the following before execution:

1. **Decision:** the current question, authorized outcome, and distinct plausible
   answers, including no correction or removal. Identify the expensive or crude
   behavior a proposed check replaces. When ordinary agent judgment still runs,
   describe the check as an addition and test its added value.
2. **Hypotheses:** each predicted observation and what would count against it.
3. **Inputs and labels:** fixed incident-derived or synthetic cases, intended
   behavior, label source, missing information, and intent amendments. Keep
   evaluator labels separate from candidate inputs. Qualify the actual context
   source before model testing: what is available at the first judgment, its
   origin, roles and order, relevant referents, proposed effect, and target
   state. Preserve current approval, amendment, and withdrawal when relevant.
   A native role label alone may not establish operator origin. A proposed
   context-complete arm needs a bounded, observed way to obtain that context;
   fixture-authored approval summaries do not establish one. Treat missing,
   malformed, truncated, or inaccessible context as an explicit limitation or
   abstention, with its fallback specified and measured.
4. **Arms and controls:** current behavior, plausible narrower/replacement
   behavior, ordinary harness behavior without the disputed check, and a
   suitable deterministic check. Keep omission eligible. State why an arm is
   inapplicable rather than silently omitting it. Specify the exact intervention
   removed; removing a supervision note differs from removing a safety veto.
   Give deterministic checks a supported input boundary and a fallback when
   they cannot decide. Supply each candidate the available context its criterion
   requires before its first judgment; if that is infeasible, record the arm as
   unavailable. Retain the current integration's actual inputs as the baseline.
5. **Identities and execution:** source/build, harness/model, prompt/configuration,
   dependencies, order, repetitions, record preservation, and supported route.
6. **Observations:** emitted output, judgments, delivered context, tool actions,
   effects, actual questions, errors, and skipped paths needed for the claim.
   Distinguish a check's answer to a supplied proposal from a complete native
   task: the former does not show that an ordinary agent would make that
   proposal or that the check prevents an additional mistake.
7. **Whole-task accounting:** measure preparation and refresh, context retrieval
   and selection, every model and Sys1 attempt, cache reuse, downstream agent
   work, retries, failure, operator interruptions, and manual recovery through
   each terminal outcome, including correct non-action. Report one-time setup
   and maintenance separately from recurring episode costs, with explicit
   assumptions for any amortization. Retain attempt identities and missing measurements;
   absent usage is unknown, not zero. Verify cumulative endpoints and cache or
   reasoning breakdowns before aggregation. A successful-call ledger alone may
   omit failed attempts or repeat historical usage on cache hits. Report
   operator effort, expensive-model usage, latency, and money separately;
   reported tokens or list-price estimates do not establish quota or billing.
8. **Acceptance and limits:** obtain the separate safety and task-alignment
   requirements, cost priorities, and permitted trade-offs from the owning
   decision. Keep case-specific thresholds there. Fix comparison fairness,
   untested boundaries, and the decision after results before execution.

Keep native controls and action permissions under their existing owners.
An arm that needs a changed permission or live configuration waits for that
specific authority. Synthetic fixtures should exercise the relevant path
without executing the real incident's consequential action.

Record both outcome dimensions explicitly:

| Dimension | Examples of observable outcomes |
| --- | --- |
| Safety | An action outside the supplied authorization is withheld; an authorized action is incorrectly stopped; result unmeasured |
| Task alignment | Supplied choices permit progress; a missing choice prompts a question before the dependent result; amended intent is followed; result unmeasured |

Observe task alignment independently of the safety result. Count withheld
output separately from a useful request for missing input. A quiet hook may
have skipped assessment; require the request, recorded verdict, and native
delivery evidence when claiming quiet handling of a judgment.

Preserve failed and skipped attempts. An attempt's failure may require a new
runner or input before retrying, with a distinct record. Changed candidate or
evidence dependencies invalidate the affected review and qualification.
Injected judgments test the integration around those judgments, not model
accuracy. One episode per cell supports a bounded observation, not a rate or
general equivalence claim.

After the run, reconcile observations with the frozen hypotheses and criteria.
First determine which arms meet the accepted reliability requirements, then
compare their complete costs under the agreed priorities. A cheaper individual
call does not establish a cheaper workflow. Reject or narrow an intervention
when its added work buys no supported benefit; retain a replacement when the
evidence supports its reliability and whole-task value. Do not invent a trade-off
the operator has not accepted.

Report each case and harness before aggregates. Return the eligible arms,
observed outcomes, cost components and unknowns,
procedure and evidence revisions, and the best-supported conclusion with its
limits. If the evidence does not choose an arm, name the smallest discriminating
follow-up or the consequential decision that remains open. Diagnostic fixtures
do not establish population reliability or authorize live adoption.
