This preparation follows the [accepted family purposes and bars](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/339#issuecomment-5961665316) and the [decision to settle the bounded commit-message cycle without advancing an assessment](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/404#issuecomment-5976049450). These facts do not establish a future installed build’s identity or invocation.

The two source lanes ran independently and join here. For SessionStart, the next consumer is an observation-scope decision covering actual delivery, relevant consumer actions, ordinary discovery, information retrieved when needed, and omission. For Stop, the next consumer must establish actual completion claims versus pauses or handoffs, current amendments, required verification, and task-owned change coverage. Any Stop variant also joins with the existing PostToolUse bookkeeping evidence; omission of worker assessment must not silently discard Stop’s baseline producer.

The maintained `handling-sys1-incidents` comparison contract applies unchanged: context before judgment, observed ordinary work before replacement claims, separate delivery and task outcomes, and complete costs with unknowns explicit. The reports name proposed observation prerequisites, not accepted runnable experiments. Research-agent usage is research cost; this increment starts no experimental native episode or Jev request and runs no hook. Startup work, recurring context, service attempts, interruptions, and downstream model work remain to be measured for any selected comparison. No economic ranking, live change, or upstream submission is established.
