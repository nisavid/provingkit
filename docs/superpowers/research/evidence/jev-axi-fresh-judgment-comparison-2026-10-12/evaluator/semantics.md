# Evidence-relation meanings

Use the four supplied relation definitions and these already-established interpretation rules. Assess evidential warrant, including when the assertion says that reports establish a fact: a derivative assertion adds no independent warrant beyond its supplied cited basis and qualifications. A statement that a fact is unestablished limits warrant; it does not, by itself, establish an incompatible event or state of affairs. Conflicting independent factual accounts that the supplied context cannot resolve remain unresolved. Respect the complete assertion scope; decide neither global truth nor an action.

These rules are part of the existing comparison's accepted meaning, not a proposed label or new question treatment.
