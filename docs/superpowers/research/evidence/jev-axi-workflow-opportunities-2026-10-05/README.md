## Proposed next observation

Prepare a separately accepted contract around one genuine, independently authorized documentation/research publication. Its first purpose is to observe ordinary work and the inputs available at each decision. Do not introduce a review step merely to remove it later or choose a workload that guarantees a defect.

| Decision | Evidence to observe | Plausible benefit to test later |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commit the staged research claim | Current request and amendments, index identity, cited evidence, prior review coverage, actual checks, unresolved questions, and final message | Necessary correction ordinary review misses; actual review work replaced; or less operator evidence hunting |
| Publish the outgoing changes | Every intended and actual ref update, full outgoing range, applicable obligations, completion evidence, actual publication route, and result | Earlier discovery of necessary work; less repeated review or obligation discovery; or less operator verification effort |

Record successful unaided paths, questions, rereads, repeated checks, failures, skips, retries, and deliberate inapplicability. Preserve unknowns where records do not expose behavior. One observation can establish an opportunity or defeat a premise; it cannot qualify reliability or comparative savings.

Deterministic identity, presence, path, and ref checks may organize evidence and expose stale bindings. They do not establish semantic support for a claim or settle ambiguous policy applicability. Add a narrow Jev comparison only where a consequential residual question and an available, costed context producer justify it. Give collection and presentation their own attribution. A routine agent step becomes a replacement candidate only after it is observed and can actually be removed without reducing quality.

This documentation case does not qualify credential protection, security review, plugin changes, release readiness, or multiple-ref handling. Those purposes retain their own evidence and acceptance needs. The [accepted family bars](../../2026-10-02-jev-axi-remaining-family-acceptance.md) and [exploratory portfolio](../../2026-10-04-jev-axi-exploratory-opportunities.md) govern any later proposal: critical cases pass, no added false interventions or loss of quality, and useful native effects, verified work removed, or measured operator benefit under its separate bar.

Staged and push preparation can continue concurrently. Join their proposals on the same candidate, actual outgoing range, prior-review scope, and evidence dependencies before selecting a shared observation. A relevant change must reach both proposals; an unrelated gap need not stop the other.
