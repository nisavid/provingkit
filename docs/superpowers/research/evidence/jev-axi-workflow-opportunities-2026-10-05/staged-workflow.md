- **Deterministic preparation:** compare staged identities with retained review identities, identify changed evidence dependencies, and present existing references. These operations can detect stale bindings or missing records, but cannot decide whether a source supports a semantic claim.
- **Omission:** add no staged assessment or presentation. An ordinary successful episode with no unmet consumer need counts against adding work.

- **Operator presentation:** an accessible evidence index may reduce Ivan's checking effort without removing agent work. Its own production cost and usefulness require measurement; Jev receives no credit for deterministic collection.
