# Parallel discovery, joined preparation

Discovery and source preparation can proceed in parallel without discarding useful findings. They are not independent end-to-end: lessons about evidence availability, current intent, retrieval, and costs must feed back into every affected proposal. Preserve the three first-pass reports; do not rerun them. The research ticket already specifies this pattern.

## Accepted criteria

The [exploratory portfolio](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/blob/450e3556c803182569c8b85f19907aaf499bcd5a/docs/superpowers/research/2026-10-04-jev-axi-exploratory-opportunities.md) permits measured reduction of operator verification or resumption effort, with trustworthy evidence, no unnecessary questions, and no task-quality loss. Agent-facing additions require useful native effects; replacements must remove actual work. Successful workflows remain eligible research material. No observed failure is required to propose an investigation.

Purpose, consumer, delivery point, implementation, and authority are separate choices. No hook, Jev assignment, experiment, or live change follows from this join. The following recommendations are provisional design suggestions, not additional acceptance criteria.

## What carries over

**Completion evidence and continuity share facts, not necessarily a document.** Both need current amendments, candidate/check identities, accessible evidence, and unresolved work. Completion asks whether a claim is supported; resumption asks what remains useful for the next decision. A completed JSON change and an unfinished later sorting request can coexist. Carrying only the final completion message forward could lose that distinction. Conversely, copying every historical failure into a handoff could trigger needless rework after successful verification. These distinctions come from the supplied completion and continuity reports, not a new inspection of their underlying runs.

**Retention and retrieval are common production work.** The continuity report describes missing original records despite surviving hashes. Identity does not supply content. Existing native records and bounded retrieval are alternatives to a new ledger. A producer must preserve or retrieve the relevant bytes, reconcile intent, select material facts, and refresh affected references. Those costs remain even when Jev receives a tidy question.

**Discovery changes the work available to save elsewhere.** Finding a suitable verification or retrieval capability may reduce completion searches and later rediscovery. A handoff may already preserve that discovery. The same avoided search cannot count as three savings. Command existence, remembered configuration, and a prior successful use also do not establish current readiness: readiness needs operation-relevant observation, with missing evidence remaining unknown.

## What must join

Before selecting concrete observation contracts, reconcile the shared source identities, requirement/amendment producers, lifecycle meanings, consumer decisions, retained references, and costs. Preserve distinct consumer views and explicit gaps; do not require one combined intervention.

Attribute benefits to collection/presentation, retention/retrieval, and any semantic judgment separately. Compare ordinary behavior, omission, and meaningful deterministic alternatives. Charge shared production once in a combined workflow, identify which standalone workflow would still incur it, and retain each consumer's marginal effort. Better context or integration is not evidence of Jev's incremental value.

Later concurrent trials could interfere through shared accounts, quotas, files, harness settings, caches, or learned task context. An earlier discovery intervention could also improve a later completion or resumption baseline. Independent cells can run concurrently where those effects are controlled or observed; otherwise sequence them or narrow the claim. This is ordinary comparison design under the [maintained comparison contract](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/blob/450e3556c803182569c8b85f19907aaf499bcd5a/.agents/skills/handling-sys1-incidents/references/comparison-contract.md), not a new review gate.

## Proposals to narrow

The completion report's suggestion to annotate all eight retained boundaries again is unnecessary for this join. Reuse the existing findings and gaps; return to a particular record only for a named unresolved question.

The continuity report's search for an actual material omission or removable reconciliation step is useful for its semantic-check or replacement hypothesis. It must not become a prerequisite for investigating operator effort reduction on a successful resumption.

All three reports leave their proposed producers and benefits unobserved. Fixed-field records, claim/evidence pairings, command menus, and readiness retrieval are candidate implementations, not established prerequisites. Synthetic contrasts can test feasibility; they cannot demonstrate native benefit. Neither missing historical measurements nor a convenient hypothetical example settles the proposal.
