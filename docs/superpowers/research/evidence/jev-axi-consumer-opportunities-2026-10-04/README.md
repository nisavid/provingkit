# Completion, continuity, and discovery opportunities

The three needs can share preparation while their observation proposals develop in parallel. Their lessons must meet before comparison contracts are frozen: completion evidence can support resumption, and better discovery can change the work either proposal appears to save. Full qualification is therefore not independent or demonstrably lossless under parallel execution.

This packet prepares [Choose the SessionStart and Stop observation scopes](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/421). ## What carries across

Completion and continuity both need current requests and amendments, task-owned change identities, verification identities, and accessible references. They ask different questions: whether a completion claim is supported, and what a resuming consumer needs next. A truthful completion record can still omit an unfinished next step. Retention makes evidence available; it does not establish that evidence's meaning or quality.

Discovery needs current intent and a consumer decision too. It may identify a verification command that completion checking would otherwise search for, or recover a route needed at resumption. The current source's command descriptions and key-presence labels do not establish an installed working route. Readiness remains unknown until the relevant operation has evidence.

These overlaps justify reusing records when their identities, availability, and claims match. They do not justify building a new requirements ledger, assuming a complete native context producer, or crediting Jev for deterministic collection, retention, and presentation.

## Dispatch and join points

1. **Parallel preparation:** develop each consumer's proposal from the same accepted criteria and source baseline. The three first passes are complete. Share useful findings now; preserving those first outputs does not require withholding later lessons.
2. **Shared-input join:** identify existing producers and gaps for current intent, candidate/check identities, lifecycle meaning, retained references, and consumer actions. Resolve changes affecting more than one proposal together; unrelated preparation continues.
3. **Contract join:** reconcile interventions, comparators, attribution, evidence retention, accounting, and shared resources before any runnable contract. Keep one proposal's intervention from silently changing another's baseline. Reusing a result also requires matching dependencies.
4. **Later execution and result join:** if separately accepted, run trial cells concurrently only where account capacity, files, settings, and observations do not interfere. An operator who has seen one presentation may learn facts that affect the next; contract design must address that carry-over. Reconcile every attempt and shared cost before claiming a benefit or selecting an assignment.

Charge a shared producer once in a combined workflow, and retain each consumer's marginal cost. Also retain standalone costs where a candidate would be used alone. Report overlap and uncertainty rather than adding savings that may describe the same avoided work. Preparation, refresh, retrieval, recurring context, failed attempts, operator effort, downstream work, and maintenance all remain in scope; unavailable measurements are unknown.
