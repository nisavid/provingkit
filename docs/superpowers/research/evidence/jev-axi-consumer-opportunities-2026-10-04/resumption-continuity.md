# Resumption continuity: first-pass opportunities

A durable, retrievable handoff could reduce rediscovery even when ordinary work succeeds. The retained evidence supports investigating that benefit; it does not establish a useful Jev judgment, measured savings, or a hook assignment.

## Grounding and limits

The [accepted exploration][portfolio] permits continuity at compaction, explicit handoff, task completion, or on demand. Operator resumption effort may be the primary benefit, provided evidence is trustworthy, effort decreases, unnecessary questions do not increase, and task quality is preserved. Agent-facing additions still require native benefit; replacements require observed work actually removed.

**Retained lifecycle fact:** the [audit][audit] found 88 ordinary and 79 representative selected original files missing at resumption. Their temporary experiment roots were absent; the cause is unknown. Thirty-four surviving inputs matched; 22 JSON records were recovered with original hashes, but no complete hook record, native stdout log, or final project file was recovered. Hash identities alone did not preserve content. This warrants storage and lookup investigation, not a classifier.

**Retained boundary facts:** the [ordinary report][ordinary] describes a final sorting projection retaining initial CSV and latest sorting requests while omitting the intermediate JSON amendment. Artifacts retained JSON, and no assessment occurred. Failed parser commands preceded successful verification. The [representative report][representative] records successful CSV and JSON boundaries followed by a controller-censored sorting turn; process exit zero did not establish whole-task success. These distinguish current intent, historical failures, and unfinished work without inventing baseline mistakes.

**Source-derived limits:** [Stop preparation][stop] traces first/latest prompt extraction, bounded diff/output, absent structured completion/pause/handoff meaning, and dependence on PostToolUse baseline production. Those inputs cannot automatically become a reliable resumption record.

No supplied report observes a real handoff/resumption pair, its consumer reading a handoff, repeated unsuccessful attempts after resumption, time spent rediscovering state, or operator effort saved. The old audit's recommendation does not constrain this later exploration; its missing-evidence limits remain.

## Proposal 1: minimal retained record with verified references

**Consumer and decision:** a resuming agent or operator deciding what remains, which evidence can be reused, and what must be rechecked. Predicted benefit is fewer searches and repeated checks, including successful resumptions that otherwise require reconstructing context.

**Decision-time inputs and producer:** ordered requests/amendments, current intent, candidate identity, completed checks and their evidence references, unsuccessful attempts with later disposition, explicit pause/handoff meaning, unresolved work, and the next decision. The active agent supplies a concise handoff from existing native records; deterministic capture preserves referenced content, identities, availability, and lookup location. Semantic reconciliation and writing remain paid agent work. Avoid requiring a new shared ledger.

**Comparators:** ordinary native resumption and existing handoff; a fixed-field record with deterministic reference/identity checks; references retrieved only when needed; omission of the extra record while retaining the ordinary baseline. No Jev judgment is necessary to retain bytes, detect a missing reference, or find a matching revision.

**Contrasts:** legitimate unchanged-candidate resumption with usable check evidence versus changed candidate or inaccessible evidence; unresolved controller-censored sorting versus completed JSON; an obsolete failed command versus a still-open blocker. A completed check must not be discarded merely because an earlier attempt failed.

**Defeat condition:** the record is unused, stale, incomplete, or costs at least the reconstruction work avoided; it creates unnecessary questions or lowers task quality.

**Cheapest informative observation:** later accept one ordinary pause/resumption pair with bounded passive observation. Record the existing handoff production, searches, rereads, repeated commands, questions, and evidence actually reused. Include a successful task. That establishes whether removable effort exists before comparing delivery alternatives; it need not induce a mistake.

## Proposal 2: bounded coverage judgment over supplied handoff entries

**Consumer and decision:** the agent preparing a handoff deciding whether its supplied entries omit a material fact needed for the stated next decision. Predicted benefit is less semantic reconciliation or a more useful handoff. Treat an extra check as an addition unless a distinct ordinary reconciliation step is observed and removed.

**Inputs and producer cost:** a bounded, source-linked current requirement sequence; the candidate handoff; supplied candidate facts about checks, amendments, attempts, and unresolved decisions; candidate/evidence identities and exclusions. Native retrieval and the agent produce these inputs. Jev cannot recover absent evidence or generate the reliable handoff. Charge selection, reconciliation, candidate generation, and refresh.

**Possible typed judgment:** for each supplied fact, choose represented, materially omitted for this next decision, superseded, or insufficient context. A later accepted contract must establish labels and fallback; ambiguity about current intent warrants ordinary reconciliation, not invented state.

**Comparators:** ordinary handoff authoring/review, deterministic required-field and identity checks, Proposal 1 without semantic checking, and omission. Jev must add value beyond the deterministic integration.

**Contrasts:** the still-current intermediate JSON amendment versus obsolete CSV; the unresolved later sorting turn versus earlier completed turns; successful verification superseding a failed command versus genuinely unfinished verification; complete versus truncated context. These are retained examples or proposed contrasts, not observed Jev successes.

**Defeat condition:** supplied context cannot establish materiality, ordinary reasoning already settles it cheaply, judgments add irrelevant history or miss current facts, or preparation/attention costs erase savings.

**Cheapest informative observation:** first inspect an accepted handoff/resumption pair for an actual material omission or separately removable reconciliation step. If neither exists, retain the hypothesis without manufacturing either. Fixture feasibility can precede native comparison but cannot establish benefit.
