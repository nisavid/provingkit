# Inspect one genuine resumption for continuity opportunities

This contract scopes a retrospective inspection of one real repository-configuration task's resumption. It asks whether retained records show adequate continuation for a named decision, identify a concrete reconstruction or reference problem worth investigating, show necessary revalidation or new work, or leave the distinction unresolved. It prepares the workload decision in [Choose the next passive observation’s workload and supported claims](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/441).

The operator selected this resumption for preparation. **Collection requires separate acceptance of this contract and its private binding.** No selected-chat history or work artifacts were read during preparation.

## Question, workload, and possible results

An authorized compact status snapshot reported that the selected agent was reconciling saved drafts and worker results before continuing two unfinished contracts. Its public tasks are [Bind the alpha to maintained source and an installation contract](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/375) and [Define the alpha's behavioral qualification contract](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/376). Both include substantial new decisions. Reported reconciliation is a reason to inspect the episode, not evidence of waste.

The consumer is the agent deciding which earlier decisions and evidence remain useful and what work comes next. The study starts from retained records after resumption began. It does not claim live observation of that start or assess the security merits of the underlying configuration work.

Classify individual activities; an episode may contain several kinds. Keep summary reports separate from facts supported by returned tool-output excerpts. Neither is a complete recording.

| Possible observation | Evidence required for that interpretation | Effect on the next decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adequate ordinary continuation for a named decision | Applicable obligation, usable handback or reference contents, evidence of relevant use, and a consistent action or outcome. | Retain ordinary practice for that decision; this observation supplies no reason to add a component. Success alone does not prove the handback caused it. |
| A concrete reference problem | Identified reference and expected content, an actual lookup result, and its time and access scope. | Consider retention or retrieval for that reference. A missing summary entry is not a missing reference. |
| Possible reconstruction of settled material | A named earlier decision and a reported or observed activity apparently reconstructing it. | Name the exact missing evidence that could confirm or defeat an avoidability hypothesis; propose a targeted join only if it could change the next decision. |
| Supported avoidable reconstruction | The earlier decision, continued applicability, a usable reference accessible at decision time, actual reconstruction of the same material, and evidence excluding relevant change, required revalidation, or new work as its purpose. | Identify a candidate activity for a later comparison. This study does not measure savings or remove that work. |
| Necessary revalidation or new design | The activity tied to a changed dependency, freshness requirement, or unresolved current obligation, with the relevant result. | Preserve necessary work and exclude it from proposed continuity savings. Unchanged bytes alone do not establish reusable evidence. |
| Unresolved or missing event | Any necessary link is absent, truncated, conflicting, or outside the permitted sources. | State the precise gap. Do not widen collection, manufacture an event, or infer general uselessness. |

Missing justification does not make a check unnecessary. A successful outcome does not establish all requirements were met. The inspector must not infer omitted actions, human origin, authority, or amendment applicability from role labels or silence.

## Source and bounded acquisition

The private binding fixes one chat, host, resumption turn, scout timestamp, exact request parameters, and a new local destination. The current `mcp__codex_app__read_thread` interface exposes recent status and turn summaries, older-page cursors, optional truncated tool outputs, and a per-item character setting. Its schema does not promise complete original messages, a total response-size bound, a starting-turn selector, producer/build correspondence, or usage counters. The [current recording research](../jev-axi-current-recording-source-2026-10-05/source-mapping.md) preserves these limits.

After acceptance, use at most **four sequential calls**, each with `turnLimit: 2`, `includeOutputs: true`, and `maxOutputCharsPerItem: 6000`. The first call omits `cursor`; each later call uses only the immediately preceding response's cursor for older turns. Keep the bound chat and host unchanged. Make no retry, enlarged request, separate status probe, polling pass, alternative source read, or worker message.

This requests at most eight turn summaries. It does not guarantee eight distinct turns or sufficient coverage. The reader starts with recent turns, so acceptance includes incidental delivery of newer or adjacent material from the same chat while locating the bound episode. Interpret only the selected resumption and antecedents needed for its handback, current request, and relevant amendments. Record that excluded material arrived without reproducing its contents in the study's analysis.

The call count, arguments, and cursor chain are enforceable request limits. **There is no supported pre-response byte ceiling.** Measure each result as UTF-8 `JSON.stringify(result)` plus one newline after it arrives; this is a local representation size, not network traffic or underlying recording size. Save complete returned JSON only while the cumulative saved responses remain at or below **1,048,576 bytes (1 MiB)**. If the next response would exceed that cap, save its acquisition metadata only, mark its content unavailable for analysis, and stop. This post-response limit does not erase content already delivered to the harness or cap harness retention. A separate acquisition ledger is capped at 65,536 bytes; it contains call parameters, identities, counts, sizes, timings, and dispositions, not copied response bodies. If metadata cannot fit, preserve a bounded failure entry and end collection; never discard earlier entries to continue. These caps cover the added response and ledger files, not the separately recorded preparation, analysis, or review documents.

Complete saved responses can contain incidentally delivered turns and embedded artifact excerpts. Keep them private. The selected span limits analysis and quotation, not what those complete response files retain. The character setting does not bound the number of items or necessarily the summary text. Tool excerpts also need their invocation/result linkage and candidate identity before supporting those particular facts; the setting supplies none of that proof by itself.

No local recording, selected worktree artifact, linked document, child chat, account file, or database may be opened through this grant. Tool-output excerpts may themselves contain artifact contents; they remain excerpts delivered through the bound chat. References may be recorded for a later precisely scoped proposal, but are not permission to follow them. Such a proposal must name the actual reference, the missing fact, the interpretation it could change, and the exact read boundary. A general wish for more context is insufficient. No additional model experiment, Jev request, live hook change, or upstream submission is included.

## Admission, endpoint, and terminal rules

Record each attempt and its parameters before calling. Preserve the returned identity, ordering, page cursors, duplicates, truncation indicators, latest returned turn, and status where exposed. Require the bound resumption turn to be identifiable before treating content as selected-episode evidence. Compare the reported start time when available. Missing fields are gaps; conflicting chat/turn identity or time prevents that join and ends acquisition. A returned newer turn never substitutes for the bound turn.

Locate the antecedent handback and applicable request/amendments through the same bounded pages. A handback without the material amendments can support a description, not an avoidability finding. Returned pages may reflect changing state; do not infer a consistent snapshot or select whichever duplicate best supports a hypothesis. Retain conflicts as unresolved.

Stop at the first of:

- The bound turn and the necessary identifiable antecedent handback/request context have been returned, including when they show the question cannot be resolved from this source.
- Four calls, the saved-response cap, missing or repeated cursor, no older-page progress, identity conflict, refusal, tool error, unusable response, or material truncation that defeats the join.

There is no substitute workload or automatic extension. Stop reading older history once adequate antecedents are found. Analyze only retained permitted content. Preserve the terminal cause and every attempted call, including failure and partial results.

The endpoint is the bound turn's status and available handback at acquisition. If it is ongoing, status is missing, or a terminal outcome is not available, label the study interim or outcome unavailable. A completed turn does not establish completion of both public contracts. Do not wait for or cause further work under this contract.

## Evidence, costs, and review

For every proposed finding, retain its call/turn/item reference where available, the relevant returned content, its status as summary or tool-output excerpt, and the missing proof links. Preserve original returned summaries as summaries; do not rewrite them as an original transcript. Digest saved response files and record selection decisions. Conflicting, duplicate, shortened, and excluded material remains visible in the ledger. Do not store hidden reasoning as a research input or publish private content.

Record collector wall time, attempted calls, returned and saved representation sizes, omissions, and preparation/selection/review work. Separate these research costs from ordinary resumed work. Historical reading, searches, rechecks, operator effort, model usage, cache reuse, money, quota, and off-chat actions remain unknown unless the retained source supports a narrower fact. Unknown is not zero. A voluntary operator record may be incorporated if naturally supplied; do not interrupt the selected task or solicit a new record under this contract.

This study supports bounded descriptions and specific next-research decisions. It cannot establish causal benefit, replaced model work, savings, a population error rate, security efficacy, or an economic ranking. A future comparison must include ordinary behavior, omission, and meaningful deterministic retention/retrieval or organization before assigning a residual semantic judgment to Jev.

Once collection terminates, outcome interpretation and acquisition/accounting review may run concurrently on the same preserved local package through leader-owned native children. They may read only that package and this contract, make no further collection or external call, and return findings to the coordinator. Join both reviews before any disposition or sanitized publication. If the package contains material outside the selected ordinary research scope, exclude it from research-child inputs and retain an explicit coverage gap.
