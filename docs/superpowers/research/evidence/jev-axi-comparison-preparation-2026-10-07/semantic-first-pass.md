# Compare earlier claim correction with ordinary authoring

This preparation makes a small public claim-support comparison ready for a local prototype. Its consumer is the research author deciding whether to retain, reread, qualify, or correct a material assertion before required final review. The candidate adds a narrow relation judgment to ordinary authoring. It earns a role only if the complete workflow improves that decision at acceptable cost.

The proposed comparison preserves three serious choices: ordinary adjacent reading, deterministic preparation with author interpretation, and identical preparation with a Jev judgment. Omission means retaining ordinary authoring and its required reviews. No experiment, service request, native observation, integration change, or behavior assignment was performed here.

## Producer: preserve the claim and retrieve its sources

For this prototype, the supported unit is one designated assertion in an existing paragraph or list, with one or two citations to the finite public source set above. The author supplies the assertion and citations as part of writing. The prototype does not find every material claim or certify the document.

1. Preserve the assertion verbatim and its enclosing paragraph or list, including the sentence carrying its citations. Preserve its draft identity. For the real correction, use first-pass lines 15–21, with line 17 designated as the assertion. Do not replace it with an evaluator's explanation.
2. Resolve the citation at its pinned revision and verify its expected digest. Retrieve the whole cited file in source order, including qualifications and reference definitions. Do not select only a favorable excerpt or recursively follow links.
3. Keep all cited sources separate, in citation order, with their identities, line numbers, and retrieval status. Candidate packets contain literal source bytes, not a summary of what they establish. The ranges above are navigation aids; all available arms receive the same complete files.
4. Check direct quotations by exact Unicode text matching, allowing only line-ending normalization. Report matched, unmatched, or no direct quotation. A mismatch is not a fabrication finding. Paraphrases require interpretation.
5. If a source is inaccessible, its digest differs, a citation cannot be resolved, or a packet is truncated, mark that condition explicitly. Do not ask the model to compensate for it. The author continues ordinary source inspection or leaves the assertion qualified or unresolved.
6. Any changed assertion, source bytes, context, or question definition invalidates the associated result. The author owns new evidence selection and correction.

This rule is reproducible without first deciding whether the assertion is true. It deliberately trades additional reading and input volume for preserving the source's qualifications. The largest source pair in the matrix is the first pass plus the push report: 25,544 UTF-8 bytes before packet metadata. The real correction uses 11,315 source bytes. These are measured file sizes, not token estimates or service-limit assurances.

The initial implementation needs only pinned Git reads, digest checks, citation resolution, line rendering, and literal quote matching. Whole-file retrieval avoids a semantic passage selector for these short records. Larger documents, more than two cited files, unresolved external references, and arguments requiring uncited evidence are outside this prototype's supported producer. Report their occurrence; do not score their exclusion as successful support checking.

Selecting the assertion still costs author attention. If the author settles its support while preparing it, that is ordinary work already completed. The semantic call then has no demonstrated remaining correction to make.

The conceptual question for an intact packet is:

> Considering only the supplied sources, what relation does the designated assertion have to their evidence? Preserve distinctions between the reported interval and later events, a candidate and an actual operation, retrieval and avoided effort, and a supplied record and broader coverage. Do not decide global truth or recommend an action.

Use mutually distinguished outcomes:

- **Supported:** the supplied evidence warrants the complete assertion at its stated scope.
- **Contradicted:** a supplied source explicitly states an incompatible fact, and the supplied sources do not conflict about it.
- **Unsupported extension:** the assertion claims more than the supplied evidence establishes, without a demonstrated incompatible fact.
- **Unresolved:** missing referents, conflicting sources, or insufficiently interpretable context prevent choosing the other relations.

A source that does not establish an event is not proof that the event never occurred. When sources conflict, the author resolves their relevance and authority; the classifier does not silently select a preferred source.

This is a proposed question, not a tested prompt. No confidence threshold, price, latency, model reliability, or SDK integration claim follows. ## Compare complete workflows

| Arm | What the author receives and does | What it can establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary authoring / omission | Existing draft, normal citations, adjacent reading, and required final review; ordinary navigation remains available | Baseline correctness, correction timing, and actual work |

| Jev addition | Exactly that packet and deterministic output, plus the proposed relation judgment; the author retains interpretation and edits | Incremental value or harm attributable to the added judgment |
| Same-question ordinary-model control | The identical packet and relation question answered through an authorized ordinary model route | Whether the narrow task needs Jev specifically; this is separate from unaided authoring |

The current jev-axi integration is not shown here to consume these claim packets or deliver this authoring assistance. A Jev service result would qualify only the tested service question. A future jev-axi adapter would separately need evidence of actual inputs, delivery, freshness, failure handling, and author use. Neither layer may claim the other's success.

Hypotheses remain falsifiable:

- Earlier checking could correct unsupported assertions before they spread into a synthesis or review package. It loses this explanation if ordinary authoring already corrects them at the same stage.
- Deterministic preparation could supply most of the value. If it produces equivalent corrections with less total work, the semantic addition should be omitted.
- A semantic hint could reduce later rework while retaining every required review. If the author and reviewer simply repeat the same work, the addition has not shown that benefit.
- Correctly bounded claims could be spoiled. Any induced overqualification, mistaken edit, redundant stop, or unnecessary operator question counts against the candidate.

### Then test the author decision

If feasibility remains useful and execution is separately accepted, begin at a genuine draft checkpoint before required final review. Preserve the original assertion, ordinary citations, prior author reads and revisions, then apply the frozen producer. Do not inject a known wrong sentence into a native task and describe its correction as prevention.

An initial shadow observation can establish whether normal writing supplies the supported inputs early enough. It cannot show an intervention effect when the author never sees the output. A later prospective comparison must show the output in its assigned arm and observe author decisions through final review. Use independent comparable authoring episodes or fresh authors, rather than replaying a learned answer to the same author. Match task scope, access, harness, model, and required reviews; report remaining differences.

Useful earlier correction requires a supported change after delivery, before the ordinary review correction, with its downstream effects retained. An author's assertion that the hint helped is useful evidence of reception, but does not by itself establish comparative savings. Required review can still find the same defect in the ordinary arm; the candidate may earn value through less downstream revision. Measure that difference. If neither arm produces a material error, record a negative opportunity result rather than force a benefit case.

The smallest native factual question is: **Does ordinary authoring expose a cited assertion before the author has already resolved its evidentiary scope, so that an added judgment can change useful work?** Answering it needs one bounded authoring checkpoint, not new private-history collection. It is not a prerequisite for completing this preparation.
