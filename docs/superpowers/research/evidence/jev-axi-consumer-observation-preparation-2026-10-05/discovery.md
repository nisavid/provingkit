## Smallest genuine observation proposal

Observe one naturally occurring unfamiliar-task discovery decision through its resulting task outcome. The agent may locate a file, retrieve specific help, use ordinary search, or skip an unavailable route. Preserve successful unaided work. Do not manufacture an orientation step, wrong proposal, failure, or pause.

Retain current request/guidance, ordered discovery commands and outputs, the chosen path, actual questions, resulting change, and applicable verification. Count searches, catalog/help reads, repeated reads, abandoned paths, failed attempts, and time to a useful next action. Judge usefulness against current requirements and the final supported result, without requiring the shortest path or a preferred command.

The first observation locates an opportunity; it establishes neither a rate nor a counterfactual saving. Later eligible comparisons are ordinary discovery, a deterministic lookup using explicit command/path/task terms, a compact relevant reference retrieved on demand, and omission of added information. Retain recurring full status as the existing integration alternative only when its delivery and side effects have a separately supported route. Deterministic rules must fall back to ordinary discovery when terms, freshness, or evidence are insufficient. No Jev arm is selected.

Relevant contrasts are explicit commands versus unfamiliar choices; relevant versus irrelevant guidance; fresh versus stale guidance; available versus unavailable operations; and useful on-demand detail versus already sufficient context. These are contract candidates, not a selected workload or new thresholds. Accepted quality requirements still prohibit false steering, redundant stops, unnecessary questions, and final-quality loss.[5]

Before execution, the coordinator must specify the actual workload/profile and permitted effects, retain decision-time context with provenance, select applicable quality checks, and establish retention plus effort/timing instruments. Freeze labels separately from candidate inputs and review the resulting contract. A task can expose discovery and completion together; continuity can use another genuine resumption. No all-family barrier follows.
