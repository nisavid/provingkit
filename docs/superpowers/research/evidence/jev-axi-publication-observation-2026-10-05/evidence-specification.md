# Bind each decision to the evidence it uses

Staging asks whether the selected changes accurately state the research findings. Publication asks whether the full outgoing change has met its applicable obligations. They share records only where their candidate, scope, and timing match. The [accepted family bars](../../2026-10-02-jev-axi-remaining-family-acceptance.md) and [comparison procedure](../../../../../.agents/skills/handling-sys1-incidents/references/comparison-contract.md) govern later comparisons; this observation selects no assessment.

## Staged claims

Use existing contemporaneous records of the base, staged paths/blobs or index tree, changed claim, cited source, and relevant dependencies. Distinguish index bytes from working-tree bytes, especially with partial staging. Join each actual check to its input mode, covered bytes, command, result, and order. A final commit cannot retroactively identify an earlier check's inputs. Missing check-time bindings remain unavailable. Sources: [staged-workflow research](../jev-axi-workflow-opportunities-2026-10-05/staged-workflow.md) and [intent/evidence research](../jev-axi-collection-routes-2026-10-05/intent-and-evidence.md).

Reuse prior review where covered bytes, dependencies, scope, and dispositions still match. A review file alone does not prove independent execution or a distinct step that can be removed. Retain ordinary corrections, repeated checks, questions, and accurate limited claims. Observation adds no review to create a replacement opportunity.

## Publication obligations

Retain the ordinary request, ready plan, separately captured reviewed-plan digest, execution result, and terminal verification. Bind the immutable source, full destination ref, observed old ref, complete outgoing commit set, destination-only commits if any, and actual route. The planner can fetch objects and create temporary refs; invoke it for ordinary publication, not to manufacture observation evidence. Sources: [publication procedure](../../../../../plugins/versionkeeping/skills/checkpointing-and-publishing-git-work/references/publication-execution.md) and its [adapter](../../../../../plugins/versionkeeping/skills/checkpointing-and-publishing-git-work/scripts/git_publication/adapter.py).

Applicable obligations depend on the actual changed surface and owning policy. [Repository instructions](../../../../../AGENTS.md) require whitespace and surface-specific checks; [contribution guidance](../../../../../CONTRIBUTING.md) supplies the broader source workflow. Match actual check/review results to the outgoing changes rather than treating a prescribed check as a completed one.

The inspected [source](../../../../../.github/workflows/provingkit-source.yml), [disposition](../../../../../.github/workflows/source-skill-disposition.yml), and [commit-compliance](../../../../../.github/workflows/cocogitto.yml) workflows trigger on pull requests and main pushes. An ordinary branch push alone has no matching push trigger in those declarations. Versionkeeping's guarded transport uses an empty hooks directory; successful transport does not establish Cocogitto or `jev-axi` hook delivery. Separately invoked checks remain separate occurrences.

Preserve ready-but-unexecuted, blocked, reconciliation-required, verified already-current, attempted-and-verified, ref-mismatch, and post-actuation-unknown outcomes. The [executor](../../../../../plugins/versionkeeping/skills/checkpointing-and-publishing-git-work/scripts/git_publication/execution.py) distinguishes failures after actuation and does not blindly retry them. Observation supplies no retry authority.

## Consumers and alternatives

Visible consumption, an ordinary subsequent action, and a voluntary operator account are distinct evidence. Delivery does not establish reading or use. A successful unaided path or absent unmet need may count against an addition, while an absent event in a bounded window remains unobserved.

Ordinary reasoning, omission, and meaningful deterministic organization stay eligible. Identity comparisons can expose stale evidence and organize existing references; they do not establish semantic support. A later Jev comparison needs a residual judgment with available, costed context. A replacement must remove observed work, and an operator aid must demonstrate measured benefit under its separate bar.
