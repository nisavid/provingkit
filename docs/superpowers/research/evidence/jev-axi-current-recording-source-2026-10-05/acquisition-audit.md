## Proposed admission, terminal, and checkpoint method

Before dependent execution, require four separately recorded answers:

1. **Identity:** the approved route identifies the selected chat and exact candidate.
2. **Freshness:** an eligible record from the selected current workload, joined to its retained request/amendment, demonstrates current coverage. A matching filename, stat, header, or recent timestamp alone does not suffice. Missing evidence ends admission as unqualified; no fabricated event, polling, or substitute source follows.
3. **Coverage:** identify available antecedents, missing origins/referents, exclusions, boundary, and eligible event shapes. Coordinator summaries retain their derivative status.
4. **Producer qualification:** record producer metadata separately from verified source/build correspondence and counter semantics. Fresh event coverage does not qualify usage aggregation.

At terminal acquisition, retain the terminal cause/time and acceptance/candidate binding, source stat identity compared with admission, boundary/append extent, actual read traffic, partial-line/parser status, selected-span identities, and explicit missing events. Preserve replacement, unavailable identity, acquisition-limit, and unjoined-outcome results without retry. Timestamp disagreement limits interpretation. This makes the existing terminal recheck independently reviewable.

For checkpoints, freeze the necessary evidence claim before selecting commands. Reuse an adequate staged path/mode/blob manifest and checked diff, tied to the inspected index state. If an index-tree identity is required, classify its lock requirement and obtain the ordinary workflow's permitted route before execution. Keep observer-only verification separate and label its failure as a measurement effect. In this occurrence, the coordinator selected `git write-tree`, which failed at the shared index lock; it establishes no unaided failure or Jev opportunity.
