# Proposal for a discriminating narrower-judgment workload

I propose a source-family holdout comparing a direct evidence verdict with narrower judgments combined deterministically, across Jev, Decisions, Luna 6 Low/Medium/High, and Sol 6.1 Low. The first claim is feasibility on supplied evidence. Omission remains eligible for a later complete-workflow comparison. This proposal selects neither a question set nor an execution, integration, probability threshold, or replacement.

## Grounding and claim

The published comparison returned matching labels on 9/12 and 8/12 Jev cases, 7/12 and 8/12 Decisions cases, and 12/12 cases for each Responses condition under both formulations. Those twelve assertions share six documents. They are known development cases, with exposed labels and explicit cues, rather than twelve independent samples or an unseen evaluation set. The result leaves decomposition open; it does not establish that decomposition helps. Source: `docs/superpowers/research/evidence/jev-axi-fresh-judgment-comparison-2026-10-12/results.md`.

Preserve the meanings in that directory's `relations.json` and `evaluator/semantics.md`: complete warrant at stated scope is supported; an explicit incompatible fact without relevant source conflict is contradicted; unwarranted extension without a demonstrated incompatible fact is unsupported_extension; relevant ambiguity, conflict, or insufficiently interpretable context is unresolved. Disagreement irrelevant to the assertion does not make it unresolved. A derivative account adds no independent warrant beyond its supplied basis. Missing measurement does not establish an incompatible outcome. Transport failure and inaccessible evidence are operational states, not additional semantic labels.

The testable hypothesis is that decomposition corrects known distinctions and transfers to new source families without reducing reliability on matched supported assertions. The paired direct control tests whether decomposition, rather than simply another invocation or easier workload, contributes. A clean result would support a bounded feasibility claim, with repetitions describing observed stability only. It would not establish population reliability, calibrated probabilities, native usefulness, or removed review work.

## Development cases and unseen evaluation

Retain c01–c12 as a named development regression set and c13 as a controlled-unavailable input check. Use them to debug semantics, schema, and combination behavior before freezing. Count every such run and revision as development work; never present its outcome as holdout performance. Reworded assertions, renamed entities, reordered versions, or new assertions against those same source families remain development material.

For the first evaluation proposal, use six new source families with four matched assertions per family: one supported, one unsupported extension, one explicit contradiction, and one unresolved. This proposes 24 primary semantic cases, not a population sample or an accepted sample-size requirement. Each family should vary a different boundary while exercising all four meanings:

| Family focus | Discriminating contrast |
| --- | --- |
| Participant and setting scope | A recorded outcome in its observed group versus extension to a different group, an incompatible recorded outcome, and an ambiguous group reference |
| Observation versus operational use | Evidence of an observed event versus inference about a different operating period, explicit contrary event evidence, and unresolved event identity |
| Activity versus benefit | A recorded activity versus an unmeasured benefit, an explicitly incompatible measurement claim, and insufficiently identified benefit or comparator |
| Reports versus reported facts | Existence of accounts despite their disagreement, an unwarranted extension beyond them, a directly incompatible fact, and conflict material to the assertion |
| Referential completeness | A fully identified entity versus scope beyond its record, an incompatible state, and a reference covering distinct entities with different states |
| Independent versus derivative evidence | Adequate direct warrant versus repetition without added warrant, explicit incompatible evidence, and conflicting independent accounts without a resolving basis |

The family descriptions are construction constraints, not the future input text or labels. Use domains, documents, events, wording, and source organization absent from the development packet. Novel names alone do not make a family unseen. A family may use two complete sources within the current producer boundary; needing more sources is an interface expansion, not permission to drop material evidence.

The family author should receive the frozen semantic definitions and these coverage constraints, but no candidate outputs. Independent evaluators should label complete candidate-visible packets and record their grounds before model evaluation. They may reject a case whose supplied evidence cannot sustain its intended distinction. Resolve label disagreement before freezing; preserve rejected and revised construction work in accounting. This is proposed evaluator separation, not a claim that unseen cases or independent labels already exist.

Keep case IDs neutral. Do not expose expected labels, family focus, construction directives, grader explanations, or intermediate gold judgments to candidates. The coordinator should separate consumer packets from evaluator artifacts in both access and serialized input, inspect the exact serialization, and freeze their inventories separately. Neither a file name nor a local directory convention alone proves separation.

Before evaluation, freeze the question set, combination table, direct question, source selection, labels, transport encodings, schedule, repetitions, and scoring. Do not tune against holdout labels or errors. Unblinding converts that set into development evidence for the next revision; do not repair a question and rerun it while retaining the same holdout claim. General wording must not embed known case identifiers, named entities, or fixture-specific rules.

Counterbalance cues across meanings: terms such as “record,” “unmeasured,” “replacement,” and “disagreement” must occur in legitimate and incorrect assertions where their use is natural. Include evidence conveyed by paraphrase, and surface lexical overlap that does not warrant the claim. Have evaluators identify shortcuts before freeze. Preregister source-order and meaning-preserving wording variants, particularly for relevant versus irrelevant conflict and clear versus ambiguous referents. Report these as dependent variants within a family, with their own costs, not new independent samples. Freeze option order or a balanced order schedule across arms; do not select whichever order produces favorable answers. The retained TypeSafe jaggedness page specifically identifies literal reading, indirection, irrelevant state, and option order as risks.

## Arms, controls, and outcomes

Retain six endpoint/model conditions: Jev with a concrete version, Decisions with its concrete Luna selector, Responses Luna 6 at Low, Medium, and High, and Responses Sol 6.1 at Low. Freeze the selectors and verify observed returned identities. Prior successful routes do not prove current access, effective effort, or multi-question support.

For every ready semantic case and condition, propose two treatments:

1. Direct verdict: one frozen four-way question over the complete assertion, enclosing draft context, and ordered complete sources.
2. Decomposed verdict: the selected narrower question set over the same substantive evidence, with a frozen deterministic combination rule.

Give ordinary-model decomposition the same dimensions, definitions, uncertainty options, and combination rule as Jev. Do not give it a hidden four-way decision sequence or a rationale requirement absent from the Jev treatment. Retain its categorical outputs without fabricating equivalent probabilities. Encode equivalent meaning through each endpoint's supported interface; retain the exact encodings so an encoding difference is visible. Calling this an equivalent output contract does not assert equivalent model execution.

Where all questions are answerable from the same frozen state, propose one Jev multi-question request. TypeSafe's retained Choice and fan-out docs describe that interface and independent per-question evaluation. An ordinary model returning a typed vector in one request is a different execution mechanism; it may jointly reason across questions. If Decisions or another endpoint cannot return the required vector through a supported interface, retain separate question requests with independent state, or mark that encoding unavailable pending a reviewed alternative. Do not silently drop Decisions or claim identical batching. The packet documents prior single-question execution, not every endpoint's decomposition capability.

For causal attribution, compare direct versus decomposed correctness within each condition and case. Separately report actual transport counts and encodings. If different request groupings plausibly alter judgments, require a separately frozen transport-control treatment before attributing the difference entirely to question design. Do not expand to all permutations by default.

Omission has no four-way answer. At this stage it is an eligible alternative to adding the check, with semantic agreement marked not applicable. It must not acquire a perfect score because it makes no requests. Its ordinary authoring, source consultation, review, correction, and terminal outcome must be observed in a later workflow comparison. No added check is justified solely by beating a direct classifier if ordinary work already supplies the needed judgment.

Retain a deterministic input-validity control: ready source identities and literal quotation relationships are mechanically checkable within the documented producer boundary. It must not infer semantic warrant from quotation matches or absence of keywords. No adequate deterministic semantic classifier is established by this packet. A non-ready control should make zero semantic submissions and retain its reason and specified fallback; do not grade it as unsupported_extension or unresolved.

Report each case and family before aggregates: raw narrower outputs, validity, combined relation, direct relation, expected relation, paired transitions, and the cause of disagreement where evidence supports it. Distinguish model errors, insufficient packet context, combination errors, and service/schema failures. Supplementary evaluator labels for narrower dimensions require independently reviewed definitions; they cannot be invented by reverse-engineering the final relation.

Retain the all-critical-cases principle. Every selected critical case, including matched supported assertions and ambiguity, must meet its frozen expected outcome for an arm to be eligible on this workload. Wrong supported-case downgrades are reliability failures, not acceptable conservatism. Failures, missing required answers, and unattempted cases prevent a clean completed pass; report any measured fallback outcome separately. Choose the exact repetition count and critical variant schedule before execution. More repeated requests do not create independent families, and a clean small set does not supply an error-rate estimate.

## Request and cost accounting

Let N be the number of ready semantic cases including scheduled variants, R the frozen repetition count, and K the number of narrower questions. With six conditions and two treatments, there are 12NR semantic treatment cells. This is not necessarily 12NR requests. Non-ready controls and omission have separate outcome records.

For condition a, let g(a,i) be the number of supported request groups needed for decomposition on case i. Without failures or additional stages, direct requests total 6NR; decomposed requests total R times the sum of g(a,i) over all six conditions and N cases. If every condition supports one vector request, the combined count is 12NR. If one condition requires K separate independent requests, its decomposed contribution is KNR. For independent same-state questions with a supported batch size b, g is ceiling(K/b); dependent stages contribute the sum of their separately required groups. These are schedule formulas, not measured savings or claims about endpoint limits.

The actual attempt count includes all observed submissions, failures, interrupted submissions, cancellations, and any separately authorized fallback or retry. Count scheduled, reserved, submitted, completed, skipped, and unused separately. A reservation without submission is not a provider attempt. Unknown transmission or residual provider work remains unknown. Responses requesting one output vector are not K independent provider requests, but all K outputs and their output tokens remain accounted for.

For every submitted request retain serialized bytes/digest, provider usage and scope, endpoint, request ID where observed, requested and returned model, requested and explicitly observed effective effort/tier/region, response bytes/digest, status, and timing. Shared state sent in distinct requests incurs submitted bytes each time. One shared acquisition event is recorded once; it does not make repeated transport or token usage free.

Use the retained accounting formulas only with valid endpoint-specific counters and frozen applicable rates. For Responses, rate equivalent is ((I-C-W) multiplied by the input rate + C multiplied by the cache-read rate + W multiplied by the cache-write rate + O multiplied by the output rate) / 1,000,000. Jev and documented input-only Decisions valuation use I multiplied by the input rate / 1,000,000. I is total input, C cached input, W cache-write input, and O output; reasoning is included in O. Validate partitions and counter scope before summing. Missing required counters or rates leave the valuation unknown; absent output partitions remain missing even when an input-only equivalent is calculable.

No current price, effective tier, or billed saving is asserted here. Bind the accepted future price record and route assumptions. Report API-rate equivalent, attributable actual bills, provider-defined subscription usage, expensive-model usage, active effort, and latency separately. Tokens do not establish subscription quota use. The previous published rate equivalents cannot substitute for new decomposed usage.

Start the whole-invocation clock before final preparation, with credential-resolution or other earlier attributable work separately captured. Continue through acquisition/selection, source resolution, extraction/hashing/rendering, refresh, requests, parsing/combination, grading, independent review, correction, fallback, operator recovery, and handback. Record UTC for correlation and monotonic durations with clock identity. Time producer completion externally; its retained internal receipt intervals omit process setup and receipt-write completion. Measure terminal typed-answer completion separately from first byte when observed. Parent/child intervals that overlap are not additive elapsed time; attributed active work is a separate quantity.

Keep fixture production, question design, interface implementation, procedure capture, source review, and maintenance as setup or on-change work, apart from recurring per-case work. Count coordinator and evaluator work, operator interruptions and manual recovery, recording overhead, failed preparation, and unused/discarded artifacts. Freeze the allowance and termination behavior in the future contract; the retained maximum is not a new execution authorization, and ledger closure does not prove a hard bound on provider work.

Count a shared event once under its invocation identity. Any allocated arm view states beneficiaries and weights summing to one, including unallocated share; the allocation is an assumption. Standalone deployment scenarios may duplicate required preparation, but are not added to actual experimental spending. Amortize setup only under an accepted reuse assumption and stated horizon. Cache identities must include full state, question set, model, and encoding; distinguish replayed historical usage from newly incurred consumption.

The prior public report explicitly leaves complete producer wall time, preparation/review model usage and attributable bills, full active effort, operator effort, and complete workflow cost unknown. Private raw records were outside this assignment and were not read. Preserve those unknowns; do not convert them into a zero-cost baseline. Future accounting must also record unknown effective fields, missing cache/reasoning partitions, uncertain submissions, and residual provider work when applicable. Each missing component must state which comparison claim it prevents. Source: `docs/superpowers/research/evidence/jev-axi-comparison-runners-2026-10-07/invocation-accounting.md`.

## Decisions and feasibility checks before a runnable contract

The coordinator should settle the selected narrower dimensions and complete combination table, direct-control wording, output validity and abstention rules, treatment encodings, repetition and variant schedule, source-family authoring/evaluator separation, supported first-judgment packet, and cost priorities. This lane supplies constraints, not the other lane's final questions. Any probability-band proposal needs a separate development/calibration design; no threshold follows from cookbook examples or this packet.

Before admitting execution, independently review the final source revision and frozen contract, confirm each endpoint's supported output interface and retained route/usage records, and obtain the separate acceptance for the concrete experiment. Reuse prior equipment where supported inputs, behavior, and accounting match; material changes invalidate affected reviews. Do not treat published equipment, a clean fixture run, or a prepared binding as execution acceptance.

Feasibility can be falsified before provider trials if independent evaluators cannot agree on the intended meanings from candidate-visible packets; source construction requires unsupported inputs; material evidence cannot fit without unvalidated selection; the dimensions cannot produce the four relations unambiguously; or required endpoints cannot emit retainable valid outputs under the proposed boundary. Accounting gaps can also defeat an economic claim even when judgment feasibility remains testable.

A future accepted run can falsify the quality hypothesis through unchanged known errors, new supported-case downgrades, misclassified relevant ambiguity, or failure to transfer to unseen families. If direct and decomposed arms both pass, the workload shows no quality advantage from decomposition; compare observed complete costs without manufacturing a benefit. If only ordinary models pass, omission and ordinary judgment remain live alternatives. If no arm passes, revisit the question/input boundary rather than selecting the least-wrong arm under the all-critical-cases bar.

Native usefulness requires a later independently accepted workflow comparison: qualify how complete working-draft evidence is obtained before the first judgment, observe actual author proposals, compare added checking against ordinary authoring/review and omission, and account through terminal work. A classifier response cannot show that an ordinary author would make the mistake or that the check removes review. Replacement additionally requires identifying the exact intervention removed and meeting its owner's reliability and task requirements. Nothing here authorizes a native child, live hook, permission change, removed review, or operational assignment.
