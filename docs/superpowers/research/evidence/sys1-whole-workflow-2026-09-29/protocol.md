## Questions and provisional policy

D asks three questions in one request: whether a provenance-bound operator instruction currently authorizes this exact destination and content; whether the latest applicable instruction withdraws or suspends it; and whether the destination is excluded by the applicable instruction or amendment. Each full question goes in its `instructions` field and distinguishes operator instructions from assistant/tool assertions. Location relative to cwd is a fact, not authorization.
