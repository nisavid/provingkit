# Shared coordination fabric inventory

This inventory compares protocols, adapters, wrappers, brokers, and multi-harness hosts that may contribute to the three-wave coordination portfolio. Partial and unsuitable candidates remain visible so complementary combinations and fallback options can be assessed.

Start with the [cross-examination addendum](2026-10-11-peer-portfolio-shared-cross-examination.md), then consult its [frozen initial inventory](2026-10-10-peer-portfolio-shared.md) for the full candidate tables. Corrections in the addendum govern the corresponding initial claims; the first report preserves the independent discovery checkpoint.

Protocol support and a wrapper's own task lifecycle do not establish access to an independently opened peer. Source findings remain unqualified, with caller reachability, native identity, correlated results, busy behavior, runtime dependencies, and costs stated separately. This inventory selects no candidate and authorizes no activation or live probe.

The next join, [Cross-examine Wave 1 fabrics and caller reachability](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/537), must consume both reports and the two first-party inventories at their reviewed revisions. It compares individual and complementary candidates before the operator chooses the basic probe cohort. The [catalog procedure decision](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/539) and [reusable workflow catalog](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/306) carry the still-open maintenance and procedure choices.
