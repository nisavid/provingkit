# Dependency-contract equipment and integration options

Existing equipment supplies component-policy exceptions, claim-specific capability evidence, affected-evidence invalidation, independent premise review, and procedure capture. The remaining work is to connect those mechanisms to the requested contract-selection behavior without assigning every dependency decision to one plugin or treating prepared guidance as deployed equipment.

This is factual research for [Reconcile dependency-contract equipment, intent, ownership, and consumers](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/510), under [Deliver dependency-contract selection and regression practices](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/499). It supplies ownership and integration options for the human design decision. It accepts no new policy, qualifies no client, and authorizes no implementation, provider experiment, installation, or upstream communication.

## Reconcile the mechanisms before changing them

**Applicability precedes preference.** The skills-plugin example distinguishes a native repository loader from ingestion and portable discovery. A stricter requirement from a different route is not automatically an applicable requirement. Artifact Customs similarly binds a narrow component policy and consumer scope, and Rolecasting rejects transferring proof across product surfaces. The requested hierarchy belongs after this route/scope check. The present map records the hierarchy and exception requirement, but no inspected installed procedure supplies that whole general decision path. [Native-route evidence][skills-contract], [component policy][artifact-policy], [capability reference][rolecasting-capability], and [delivery charter](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/499).

**Conformance, a local guard, and a policy exception are separate decisions.** Artifact Customs forbids silently broadening a failed policy and permits attestation absence only under an expressly adopted exception with corroboration. The Git candidate deliberately chooses a narrower provenance rule than Git itself while permitting documented overrides. The release validator narrowly recognizes selected evaluator false positives. These are useful examples, but none authorizes agents to treat any passing implementation behavior as a promised contract. A design must say which promise applies, which local requirement governs, and who can change an exception. [Clearance][artifact-clearance], [candidate ADR][git-provenance-adr], and [advisory implementation][release-validator].

**Intent evidence and runtime evidence answer different questions.** The motivating compatibility reference retains deliberate upstream changes and an explicit review decision, including a stale commit-summary statement that differs from final code and tests. Its boundary tests remain required despite that history. The accepted Rolecasting policy likewise selects evidence from the actual claim: deterministic configuration, generic transport, and exact-model execution need different observations. Historical intent may explain stability; it cannot replace a current boundary result. [Compatibility reference][skills-contract] and [accepted probe policy][probe-decision].

**Reuse should preserve claims rather than multiply gates.** The accepted probe policy already binds claim, scope, dependencies, provenance, freshness, authority, and an owner, with affected-only invalidation and cross-workflow reuse when independently verifiable. Tricritical already invalidates affected reviews on candidate or material evidence changes. The component-clearance contract requires reassessment when its candidate/policy/authority binding changes. Reconcile those existing triggers before introducing another evidence record or collection service. Their scopes differ, so a shared pointer cannot silently weaken a specific owner's required rebind. [Probe decision][probe-decision], [review boundary][review-boundary], and [clearance][artifact-clearance].

**A stronger evidence mode is an explicit input.** Ordinary review can use controller observations within its stated assurance limits. Witnessed mode needs separately qualified issuance and cannot fall back when its required evidence is unavailable. Rolecasting's pure renderer and native recorder do not launch workers or authenticate facts the harness has not supplied. The accepted general regression obligation must name the actual operational claim and supported control surface; it must not turn ordinary discovery into a provider-attestation requirement. [Rolecasting package boundary][rolecasting-readme], [review boundary][review-boundary], and [dispatch qualification][dispatch-qualification].

**Cost and escalation already have useful constraints.** Probe policy counts whole costs and preserves uncertain outcomes; method research calls for matched baselines, failed attempts, holdouts, and ablations. Artifact Customs continues resolvable read-only investigation instead of escalating simply because a deviation exists. The unsupported-gate correction separately requires a concrete outside need rather than an available support route. Keep all four contributions; neither an arbitrary research duration nor a contact form is a completion or escalation criterion. [Probe decision][probe-decision], [method handoff][method-handoff], [clearance][artifact-clearance], and [unsupported-gate work][unsupported-gates].

## Ownership and integration options

These are concrete design options, not accepted allocations. The [shared decision](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/512) should compare invocation reliability, behavioral coverage, maintenance cost, and supported consumers, using the constructor's existing placement ladder where available. Its policy remains with the work it governs. [Constructor design][policy-design].

| Option | Concrete shape and benefit | Remaining decision and cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extend existing owner guidance and connect the seams. | Add the software-component branch to Artifact Customs' authored policy/clearance references; add required premise/contract questions to the appropriate Tricritical rubric; consume Rolecasting's existing evidence rules for capability claims; keep concrete compatibility tests with each actual dependency consumer. Use only the minimum pointers needed at existing entrypoints. | Decide where the shared selection reasoning is canonical and how other owners invoke it without duplicating policy. Separately maintained fragments can drift or fail discovery if the shared path is implicit. |
| Add focused shared equipment with explicit owner calls. | Establish one narrow trigger for a disputed dependency contract, a common decision result, and links from participating owner procedures. Preserve Artifact Customs clearance, Rolecasting dispatch, Tricritical review, Versionkeeping Git, and rollout actuation. | Choose the maintained owner and exact invocation contract through the human decision. A new entrypoint earns its cost only if discovery and cross-workflow use outperform the smaller reference/pointer alternative. A distinct name or schema is not settled by this report. |
| Add a bounded mechanical helper only where repetition warrants it. | Reuse patterns from exact evaluator advisories and the real Codex discovery tests for identity binding, scoped drift detection, and negative controls. Keep each dependency's actual oracle in its consumer rather than building a universal parser imitation. | Prototype whether any common helper has stable inputs and saves real work. A helper cannot infer applicability, authority, or intent merely by gathering metadata. No general evidence service or universal mandatory gate is justified by the inspected evidence alone. |

The first two options can share unchanged equipment. The third is conditional on measured repetition, not an additional required platform. Artifact Customs' component classes and outward calls should remain as declared; Rolecasting should retain model selection and evidence assurance; Tricritical should retain review independence and adjudication; policy construction and method research should retain their existing owners. The [component composition boundary][artifact-policy], [Rolecasting package][rolecasting-readme], and [method handoff][method-handoff] already support that separation.

Its preserved alpha.3 baseline is not replaced by a newer main commit. Public publication, installed-byte verification, discovery, invocation, and demonstrated benefit need their own evidence.

Keep the fork's [deployment](https://github.com/nisavid/mattpocock-skills/issues/8) and PR gates with their owner. This report neither merges that PR nor expands its compatibility claim.
- **To use broader comparison methods:** consume the [method handoff][method-handoff] as research input and the [evaluation map][eval-map] for current scoped decisions; do not manufacture acceptance of an external method or make the whole survey a prerequisite. [Shared intake](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/504) remains a neighboring correction for recognizing cross-cutting efforts, not a new global owner of this map.

These are result-specific integration obligations. They are not instructions to reparent existing tickets or add blanket blocking edges. The exact edges depend on which implementation and adoption increments the design selects.

## What the next decisions still need

Discoverable gaps include actual consumer inventories for any newly selected entrypoint, current candidate review results, exact installed/projection identities, and the supported boundary tests for additional dependency classes. Those can be investigated without asking the operator to supply repository facts.

Human decisions remain the maintained home and participating owners, the intended interpretation when applicable promises conflict, acceptable exception benefits and alternative costs, supported populations, evaluation thresholds, and the first deployment/adoption increment. The map requires coverage of research, prototypes, generalization, integration, evaluation, adversarial review/revision, ablation/optimization, and deployment. No inspected source supplies accepted thresholds or a universal mandatory evidence schema for that whole effort. [Delivery charter](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/499), [evaluation scope][eval-map], and [constructor design][policy-design].

The useful unchanged foundation is substantial: explicit scope, named authority and exceptions, claim-specific observation, ordinary-versus-witnessed assurance, affected-only freshness, independent premise review, producer/consumer capture, and actual-boundary tests. The missing result is a tested and adopted way to compose that foundation for disputed dependency contracts.
