# Qualification observation: existing producers and remaining feasibility gaps

The assessed Desktop archive supplies useful query getters and a direct worktree-registry lookup. It does not yet supply a complete, accessible observation of the accepted qualification state. Effective query account route, applicable permissions and pending changes, selected-context model relevance, and raw current cwd provenance remain unresolved. No verified public canonical Code source/build/load route was found for adding the missing producers. Full qualification planning therefore remains blocked; this research adopts no patch or narrower claim.

This report supplies the source findings for [Assess existing producers for qualification-state coverage](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/478) and [Establish qualification observer source and load feasibility](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/479), and their [comparison and review join](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/480). [Choose the next qualification increment from feasibility findings](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/481) owns the subsequent choice. The smaller fixture experiment and its [selected-directory binding decision](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/476) remain independent.

## Accepted claim and evidence boundary

The [accepted research decision](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/414#issuecomment-6053930674) preserves the [reviewed qualification design](2026-10-03-qualification-observation-design.md). Before/after qualification samples concern the selected ordinary task's:

- Applied model configuration, with observed fallback information separate.
- Effective query account, organization, and authentication route.
- Applied permissions: applicable mode, rules, grants, runtime inputs, and pending changes.
- Registered worktree and current working directory.

Saved preferences and initialization metadata are insufficient for those applied meanings. Applicability follows the selected task's actual consumers; another task's state or an unrelated fork does not become a requirement because its store exists. Conversely, a standing grant can apply without the fixture invoking its tool. No task configuration was read here, so conditional facets remain unresolved until their consumer and relevance are established.

The claim does not require server attribution of every request, proof of each backend inference model, an inventory of every context, atomic sampling, or continuous preservation. Ordinary notifications retain their separate exact-peer/consent, receiver-origin delivery, and correlated-acknowledgment contract. Full state snapshots apply to initial and affected qualification.

I integrated two independently frozen first reports and their separate cross-examinations. Both first reports were complete and hashed before either lane read the other. The [source manifest](2026-10-08-qualification-feasibility-sources/manifest.json) retains their identities and the public artifact identities. These are static source findings: no vendor code was executed, and no running Claude process, installation, profile, account, task, credential, or receiver data was read. No hook, fixture, prompt, restart, installation, or send occurred.

## What the recovered source changes

The [access first report](2026-10-08-qualification-feasibility-sources/access-first.md) recovered the exact previously assessed `claude-desktop-extra` 2.9939.4-1 archive from its versioned public packager release. Its ASAR and four retained Desktop members match their earlier digests. It also identifies the actual bundled RC SDK implementation and worktree owner. A separately recovered official apt package has the same displayed version and commit but different Manager, Host, and Common bytes. Its anchors cannot be substituted for the assessed archive's. Neither package signature was verified in this research; hash matching establishes the recorded byte identities, without a package-authentication acceptance claim.

The actual bundled SDK implements `getSettings`, `listPermissionRules`, `getStatus`, and `getContextUsage` on an owned query. Their request/response correlation is present in the assessed source. This closes the prior wrapper-method source gap; it does not prove actual replies on a selected running task or an external attachment route to Desktop's existing query. `accountInfo()` remains initialization data. Reinitializing or constructing a query would change the lifecycle rather than passively observe the existing one.

The worktree owner has a non-resolving `getWorktreeForSession` lookup. Once loaded, its body performs no Git command or registry write. It nevertheless clones and scans the whole cached registry before returning the first selected-session lease whose status is not `creating`. A selected output therefore does not imply a selected-only acquisition. The [coverage cross-examination](2026-10-08-qualification-feasibility-sources/cross/coverage-report.md) and [access cross-examination](2026-10-08-qualification-feasibility-sources/cross/access-report.md) correct the first access report's narrower footprint and singular-association assumption.

That lookup is useful for a settled registered association, subject to owner/task binding and uniqueness. Null does not establish authoritative absence: creation is filtered, storage can fall back to an in-memory pool, and a movement can be in progress. Cold initialization can have persistence/error effects. Resolving the worktree performs Git/filesystem work and may change Manager state; it must not be invoked merely to make a sample look fresh. Registered association is also distinct from the Engine's current cwd. A new worktree status producer is no longer an assumed prerequisite.

## Proposal for the next decision

I recommend retaining the full claim and its explicit blockers while selecting a bounded source continuation, rather than implementing another partial observer as though it solved qualification. The decision can choose among these concrete increments or combine independently useful pieces:

1. **Close selected-task applicability and existing-equivalent questions.** Trace main-context model/permission lifetime, admitted fallback pass-through, Desktop grants/pending categories, and worktree owner initialization/uniqueness. For account route, permission state, and raw cwd, name an existing producer or leave the exact missing facet open. This remains source work and can refine the scope of any new producer without changing the accepted claim.
2. **Prepare a producer/source-route request for the Code/SDK owner.** Describe only the unresolved selected-query fields, desired passive semantics, and source/build/load evidence needed. Preparing a reviewable request is distinct from sending it; external contact requires its own authority. An unavailable owner-supported route leaves full qualification blocked.
3. **Prepare a narrowly scoped getter-support experiment.** After fixture/directory selection and a reviewed acquisition grant, an ordinary disposable query could test the admitted access path, getter support/reply shapes, correlation, late replies, timing, and effects. It would reduce operational uncertainty for partial producers; it cannot qualify fields that have no producer. No such experiment is authorized by this report.

A decision to narrow or defer qualification belongs to Ivan and must name the removed facets and resulting claim. It is not the default consequence of this research. App instrumentation remains a conditional option for a specific gap, with temporary diagnostic and ongoing operating uses assessed separately. The evidence does not justify adopting it to close the full claim now.

## Procedure capture and downstream use

Under `capturing-agent-procedures`, this report captures a provisional repository-specific source-assessment procedure. It is research guidance, not installed operating equipment or a live observation procedure:

1. Load the current accepted field meanings and reviewed source revision. Keep specimen decisions in their owning tickets.
2. Bind artifact member hashes, source target, actual executor, selected Desktop task, and owned query separately. Do not infer one from another or carry anchors between packages by version label.
3. Trace each field to its selected-task consumer. Classify it as included, excluded with source-backed relevance, or unresolved. Name empty, unavailable, in-progress, and ambiguous results distinctly.
4. Prefer demonstrated existing producers. Record their whole acquisition footprint, effects, pending state, lifecycle, and individual intervals before proposing a new producer.
5. For a missing producer, establish a practical source/build/load or supported API route before choosing implementation. Compare ordinary-build transfer, interruption/restoration, and maintenance; do not substitute a speculative compiled-binary edit.
6. Freeze independent first findings before exchange, retain cross-examination corrections, and independently review the final unchanged proposal. A missing required field keeps full qualification incomplete.
