# Purposes and acceptance for the remaining jev-axi hook families

This record defines the accepted purposes, critical contrasts, and acceptance
bars for the remaining hook comparisons. I accepted the complete contract on
2026-10-02, including the comparison order and workload boundary.

It resolves the scope of [Define purposes and acceptance bars for the remaining
jev-axi hook families](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/339).
It does not select a behavior assignment or authorize experiments, implementation,
live changes, or upstream submission.

## Settled scope

Commit-message agreement leads. The remaining families stay in the catalog
plan, with no assumed retention merely because they are currently implemented.
The [supervision disposition](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/338#issuecomment-5959242739)
advances neither tested assessment package and retains the context improvement
as an unqualified research candidate.

Comparisons may include replacement of a named routine agent review step,
but only after that step is observed. A procedure that prescribes review is
evidence of a requirement, not proof that the step occurs or can be removed.
Ordinary review and omission remain comparators. Writing a message is not
automatically a separate review step, and a Jev check does not automatically
replace either activity.

The initial security-related scope is consequential mistakes during legitimate
work, using harmless stand-ins and matched legitimate actions. Adversarial
resistance requires a separate threat model and evidence. Neither this scope
nor a favorable score grants blocking authority.

## Workload boundary

Begin with agents conducting ordinary repository work through Claude Code or
Codex. Each runnable contract must identify its actual harness,
model, source, dependencies, instructions, hook configuration, supported inputs,
and workload. Evidence from one configuration cannot silently qualify another.
Use ordinary-language task requests; structured intent supplied only to a
candidate must be produced and costed as part of that candidate's workflow.

## Family purposes and critical contrasts

| Family and consumer | Accepted purpose | Critical contrasts required before qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| commit-msg; the agent preparing the commit | Correct a materially false claim or an omission that makes the message misleading about the committed change | Misleading claims and omissions versus accurate concise summaries; message body and intended committed scope; staged versus unstaged changes; input truncation or omitted files; supported special messages and intentional skips |
| Stop; the agent claiming completion | Catch unmet current requirements, including required tests or verification | An unmet current requirement versus completed work; a missing required check versus sufficient verification; amended requirements versus obsolete ones; completion claims versus explicit pauses or bounded handoffs; incomplete or truncated task and change context |
| SessionStart; the agent choosing tools or discovering available capabilities | Reduce discovery effort or improve useful tool selection | Relevant command/readiness information versus already-known or irrelevant information; current versus stale information; a ready service versus unavailable service; ordinary discovery and information retrieved when needed versus recurring injection and omission |
| PreToolUse; the agent proposing an action under native permissions | Prevent consequential actions that conflict with current intent | Unintended consequential actions versus similarly risky-looking intended actions; applicable, missing, withdrawn, and differently scoped authority where relevant; adequate versus missing task context; assessment errors and unavailable service |
| pre-commit; the agent reviewing staged changes | Catch actionable defects or unintended credential exposure | Defects versus deliberate behavior; genuinely required tests versus adequate tests outside filename heuristics; unintended credential-like content versus intentional harmless fixtures; accidental leftovers versus deliberate TODOs; staged versus unstaged changes and partial input |
| pre-push; the agent preparing the push | Catch required release or operational work still missing for the changes actually being pushed, beyond earlier checks | A missing required migration or operational step versus an already completed or inapplicable step; actual pushed refs versus a selected subset; new branches, deletions, repeated pushes, and prior blocked pushes; complete versus truncated ranges; a new useful finding versus a duplicate earlier warning |

These contrasts define obligations for the relevant claim, not a demand to
support every input immediately. The runnable contract must freeze exact cases,
expected behavior, grading, and exclusions before execution. An unsupported
case must be excluded explicitly from the supported claim; it cannot be counted
as a successful negative case. Source-only inspection cannot pass a runtime case.

For commit-message agreement, completeness of enumeration, stylistic preference,
subject polish, and Conventional Commits formatting are separate purposes.
They cannot count as successful semantic findings. Format compliance remains
eligible for a deterministic comparison if pursued separately.

For pre-commit, local pattern rules and semantic assessments require separate
attribution. Matching a credential pattern does not establish that a real
credential was exposed. Harmless fixtures support only their stated test claim.

For pre-push, sensitive-area labels or generic risk notices do not by themselves
establish required work. The consumer must have a useful action to take, and
the comparison must account for earlier reviews.

## Acceptance bar

Every agreed critical case must pass for the candidate's supported scope.
Compared with matched legitimate work under ordinary harness behavior, a
candidate may add no false steering, redundant stops, or unnecessary questions,
and may not reduce final task quality. A benefit requiring relaxation of this
bar returns as an explicit trade-off. Passing a bounded case set establishes
neither a population error rate nor general safety.

An added check must demonstrate a useful effect in a native task. A replacement
must preserve quality while actually removing the verified review work.
Invented failures, injected scores, and forced wrong proposals can qualify
mechanics or classification cases, but cannot establish prevention of a failure
the ordinary harness would otherwise make. Quiet diagnostic records require a
named consumer and demonstrated benefit; possible future use is insufficient.

Each comparison retains ordinary harness behavior, omission of the disputed
behavior, and meaningful deterministic alternatives. If a deterministic rule
cannot establish its premises from actual task evidence, it remains unqualified.
For SessionStart, information retrieved when needed is an explicit alternative.
For Jev assessments, context preparation must supply the information required by
the question before judgment; later recovery does not excuse missing context.

Classifications, output delivery, useful correction, final outcome, and removed
work are separate observations. Retention requires the relevant benefit and
reliability evidence. Missing benefit evidence leaves a candidate unqualified;
it does not establish uselessness or authorize removal of a live behavior.

## Cost and authority

Apply the accepted whole-workflow priorities: unnecessary interruptions first,
then constrained expensive-model capacity; report latency and money separately.
Include context preparation, discarded and unused assessments, service errors,
retries, review and recovery, operator effort, recurring context, and maintenance.
Distinguish one-time research effort from recurring workflow cost without hiding
either. Unknown billing, capacity, latency, or cost components remain unknown.
Do not claim economic superiority from partial measurements.
A lower assessment price or a passing classification test cannot establish
assignment by itself. When the demonstrated benefits and complete costs do not
yield a choice under these priorities, return the unresolved trade-off for a
decision rather than declare a winner.

Each contract must identify warning, blocking, or quiet-record behavior and its
consumer. Existing output modes may be compared where authorized; choosing a
purpose or observing a favorable classification selects no new authority.
Native permissions remain part of the baseline. A new live configuration or
broader blocking role requires its own concrete decision.

## Prerequisites, parallel work, and joins

For commit-message agreement, first establish the actual native message-writing
and review workflow, whether a separate review step exists, what context is
available at commit-msg time, and which costs can be observed. Source inspection
and bounded existing evidence can prepare these facts; any new native episode
requires a concrete accepted execution contract. Do not create review work just
to give a replacement something to remove.

The other families have their own factual prerequisites:

- SessionStart: observe the actual delivered context, startup behavior, discovery
  work, and tool choice; source construction alone establishes none of these.
- Stop: establish which current requests and amendments reach the check, when
  the change baseline is captured, and whether the event signifies completion
  rather than a pause or handoff.
- PreToolUse: establish the available intent and authority context, the native
  permission path, and whether ordinary work actually proposes a consequential
  mistake. Do not inherit a general protection claim from the earlier bounded
  file-creation comparison.
- Pre-commit: identify which local or semantic findings reach the agent and
  require action, with staged-content coverage and existing test/review evidence.
- Pre-push: establish actual pushed-range coverage, prior review findings,
  available release requirements, and repeat/cache behavior before claiming
  push-wide value.

Context/accounting investigation and reusable-method reconciliation may proceed
in parallel on this accepted scope. Reuse the existing incident intake aid and
comparison procedure where their claims still fit; record necessary changes and
their owning consumer instead of introducing an unowned method. Reconcile the
current broader evaluation-scoping policy at the protocol join. A rule or fact
that does not affect this comparison is not a blanket dependency.

Independent family preparation can proceed without waiting for the commit-message
outcome. Stop bookkeeping and baseline timing require coordination with any
PostToolUse assessment variant. Pre-commit and pre-push comparisons must account
for shared diff review and duplicate findings. Shared service calls and context
are charged once in combined workflows, with their allocation made explicit.

Join context, accounting, method, grading, source identities, and resource limits
before freezing each runnable contract. Join all retained attempts and independent
reviews before choosing an assignment. The next qualification consumer is the
commit-message comparison contract; later family contracts use these purposes
and bars with their own frozen cases and supported scope.
