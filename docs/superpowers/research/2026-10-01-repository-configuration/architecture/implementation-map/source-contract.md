## Question

How will the accepted design be maintained, identified, distributed, installed, and recovered in its selected local clients?

Consume the completed architecture and alpha-context decisions. Use `grilling`, `domain-modeling`, `codebase-design`, and `capturing-agent-procedures` to bind the selected packaging to its canonical source path and owner, descriptive identity, alpha revision/artifact identity, invocation pointer, target client surfaces, and installation/recovery route. Do not reopen accepted capability, contextual-judgment, or architecture choices without new material evidence.

Inspect the current source and deployment owners. The current projector and installer are bounded to the existing Kit; a new route must preserve that boundary or make its narrow source/validation amendment explicit. A repo-carried Skill needs a concrete source-to-global deployment contract, while an independent Plugin needs its own packaging and distribution identity. Neither automatically becomes a whole-Kit Release.

Resolve how consumers locate and invoke the reviewed revision, how shadowed/duplicate providers are detected, what prior state is retained for recovery, and where future corrections are maintained. Record the decision and reviewable source/deployment contract as the producer input to implementation. This ticket settles the route; it does not install the equipment.
