# Repository-configuration research

Start with [the synthesis](research-synthesis.md) for findings, uncertainties, and the questions carried into the separate architecture panel. [The live map](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/366) tracks decisions and their dependencies.

- [Method and provenance](method.md) describe isolation, exchange, recovery, and downstream use.
- [Historical rationale](history/round-1.md) and its [cross-examined follow-up](history/round-2.md) distinguish retained intent from observed settings.
- [GitHub mechanisms](github/round-1.md) and its [cross-examined follow-up](github/round-2.md) connect settings to behavior and applicability.
- [Conceptual alternatives](conceptual-first.md) and their [cross-examined follow-up](conceptual-follow-up.md) inform the architecture comparison without selecting its shape.
- [Coordinator observations](coordinator-observations.md) preserve dated read-only evidence about Provingkit.

First reports are frozen historical outputs, including their proposals and overclaims. Follow-ups and the synthesis own corrections. The [first-report identities](initial-reports.json), [exchange snapshot](exchanges/1/manifest.json), and [follow-up identities](follow-ups.json) bind those stages. Full third-party source snapshots and private historical extracts remain local; public citations and selected source identities are retained here.

This packet supports design decisions. It does not establish a universal configuration baseline, exercised hosted enforcement, or a deployed alpha.
