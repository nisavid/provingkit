# Active-effort continuation: reconciled evidence and discriminating comparisons

An active effort needs both a correct account of its remaining obligations and a supported way to return attention when those obligations need it. The retained cases show failures in each, useful continuation, legitimate waits, and unresolved outcomes. They do not establish a preferred coordinator lifetime, observer, event route, cadence, or economic rank. This comparison gives the Praxis decision owners the smallest concrete alternatives, their actual native limits, and the evidence that would distinguish them.

The [initial native/history checkpoint](2026-09-30-continuation-evidence-r1.md) remains the case catalog and coverage record. This synthesis adds cross-informed corrections rather than reproducing its catalog. The companion [orchestration-solutions survey](2026-09-30-orchestration-solutions-survey.md) is the intended home for the broad external inventory; the [whole-Kit method handoff](2026-09-30-whole-kit-method-handoff.md) routes methods beyond continuation. The three reports share the source and exposure boundaries recorded below.

## What the evidence permits

The [accepted domain](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/96#issuecomment-5721914053) gives Praxis effort-level stewardship. The [accepted cohort](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/144#issuecomment-5745003755) provides one workflow-independent kernel, focused procedures, and optional adapters. Portfolio triage and reconciliation are accepted procedure purposes; a permanently running portfolio service is still a choice. The [accepted kernel](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/157#issuecomment-5745374858) distinguishes effort identity, work items, participants, owners, claims, grants, obligations, observations, and typed uncertainty. Successful closeout requires every in-scope obligation satisfied, authoritatively waived, or transferred, no active claim or handoff, and no unresolved unknown effect. Its `validate`, `reconcile`, and `assess` primitives return data, holds, and proposals without external actuation. These planning contracts are fixed inputs, not an implemented or qualified kernel.

Rolecasting retains topology, model/effort selection, and dispatch; Mergecraft retains individual PR operations; Versionkeeping retains Git/worktree mechanics; Tricritical retains general review; Artifact Customs retains component lifecycle; and Proseweaving retains generic prose. Praxis selects the appropriate owner and reconciles its typed result. Ownership and a running participant do not supply an Authority Grant. Optional Wayfinder and other graph integrations preserve source state, scope, completeness, and unsupported capabilities. They do not merge distinct efforts because those efforts reference the same PR or source.

This result answers the documentary comparison in [Explore active-effort continuation designs independently](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/345) and reconciles inputs for [Establish native continuation and event capabilities for active efforts](https://github.com/nisavid/provingkit/issues/343). It distinguishes six kinds of evidence throughout:

- **Exposed schema and source/project claims:** a tool contract, official snapshot, or maintained source declares a capability; installed operation is unverified unless separately observed.
- **Inspected implementation:** a lane read a pinned operative path. Its conclusion covers that path and its semantic read extent, not the whole product.
- **Authored tests:** assertions and constructed fixtures describe the tested condition. Reading them supplies no execution result.
- **Author-reported published evaluation:** a study's result belongs to its original tasks, models, scorer, version, exclusions, and uncertainty. It is not local reproduction.
- **Retained local observation:** a dated message, receipt, receiver record, or transition is present in the frozen evidence. Participant-reported external outcomes and causes remain reported.
- **Inference and proposal:** a comparison, possible explanation, future measurement, or capture contract remains an inference or proposal until its owner supplies the required evidence and decision.
