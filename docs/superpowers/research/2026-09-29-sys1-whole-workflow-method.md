# Whole-workflow Sys1 comparison method

`handling-sys1-incidents` now asks whether a Sys1 check earns its place in the complete workflow. The [maintained comparison contract](../../../.agents/skills/handling-sys1-incidents/references/comparison-contract.md) distinguishes replacing existing work from adding another check, qualifies available context before the first judgment, and keeps ordinary harness behavior, omission, and deterministic alternatives eligible. It applies the owning decision's reliability requirements and cost priorities rather than installing this incident's thresholds as general policy.

Accounting covers preparation and refresh, context retrieval and selection, every model and Sys1 attempt, cache reuse, downstream work, retries, failures, interruptions, and manual recovery through terminal outcomes. Setup and maintenance remain separate from recurring episode costs, with explicit assumptions for amortization. The handback exposes every accounting component as measured, assumed, or unknown and reports each case and harness before aggregates. Reported usage, quota, list-price estimates, and billing remain different quantities.
