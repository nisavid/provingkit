## Comparisons and unresolved measurement questions

The simplest baseline is the existing applicable method, including one agent invoking owners sequentially where contracts permit. Required specialist routes and independent review remain requirements. Additional context packets, delegation, return trigger, persistence, shared observation, and delta suppression are separate treatments. Compare matched workloads and authority with held-outs and adverse cases; ablate one added behavior at a time. One useful scheduled return and a verified native one-shot control are different claims; a persisted checkpoint and a runnable recovery service are different prerequisites.

Define independent efforts and opportunities before selecting outcomes. Count successful, failed, abandoned, cancelled, held, unresolved, and no-change opportunities; join replacements, forks, copied context, parent/child outputs, and duplicate notices to their original effort where evidence supports the join. Report accepted whole-effort completion alongside per-obligation results. A valid worker increment can leave parent review/integration/closeout open; a delivered wake can leave the wrong next obligation selected. Conversely, a legitimate hold can coexist with useful independent work or an unavailable answer surface.

Capture authoritative actionable/due transition, observation, accepted dispatch, receiver start/acknowledgment, first useful result, reconciliation, and accepted closeout as separate clocks. Preserve origin, uncertainty, missing times, and censored outcomes. Observer-read time cannot substitute for delivery; absent process/session handles establish no cause by themselves. Include setup, tuning, infrastructure, observation/source calls, cached/uncached inference, context restoration, transport, workers, failed/repeated work, recovery, maintenance, review, and human rescue. Shared overhead needs an allocation rule. Zero accepted completions must not produce a misleading cost-per-success ratio.

Study-specific questions remain explicit. AI Agents That Matter's v1 PDF HumanEval methods use 164 modified-LDB tasks, five runs per agent, named April 2024 GPT versions/prices, and Student-t uncertainty; final evaluation tests differ from example-test stopping. Its HTML date discrepancy is unresolved. MAST's theoretical sample is not a local incidence denominator: three human five-trace refinement rounds, headline agreement, and few-shot o1 judge agreement are distinct; ten-versus-five judge-validation membership remains unresolved. AWM's successful-task steps omit failed attempts and induction/retrieval costs. The scaling paper's task/model/architecture-specific author-reported effects provide protocol questions without a portable threshold. Original task/scorer/model artifacts and exact study membership need their own audit before quantitative reuse.

## Consumer prerequisite and completion boundary

Before dependent execution, the maintained owner must accept the source/trigger/consumer contract and provide an immutable reviewed revision with current selected behavior/review evidence, applicable publication/projection status, and lifecycle disposition. The consumer loads that revision, checks supported harness/model/adapter and evidence freshness, verifies the typed prerequisite result, and records actual invocation plus output verification. Its owning workflow must carry the real prerequisite and check for dependency cycles. A report filename, primary link, or source assertion alone is insufficient.

Candidate, source behavior, fixture/scorer, harness/model, adapter, projection, notice boundary, compatibility, or consumer-prerequisite changes can invalidate evidence. Feed useful corrections to the same maintained producer; keep unsettled methods as proposals. Independent whole-Kit documentary scouting can proceed under its own charter, while dependent method execution waits for its reviewed producer result. Capture/installation and future generic promotion retain their separate owners and authority.
