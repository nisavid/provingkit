# Refine the two additional-check designs

The next inspectable experiment should separate deterministic admission,
semantic state and questions, and withheld evaluator/consumer criteria.
The candidate definitions below remove overlaps from the initial outlines.
They remain source-design proposals. Independent labeling, packet review,
the prototype reaction, calibration choices, and execution grants remain open.

## Interpretation questions

Acknowledgment describes what the admitted receiver says about the applicable
notice. It does not prove that the receiver really read or understood it.
Receiver identity, speaker role, quoted-content boundaries and correlation
come from admitted evidence; self-proclaimed provenance supplies none of them.

Use a declared precedence for mutually exclusive candidate labels:

1. `unresolved`: complete context leaves applicable self-assertion, denial or
   referent in material conflict or ambiguity.
2. `explicit_ack`: the receiver explicitly asserts its own receipt or
   understanding of the applicable notice, even when it refuses follow-up work
   or also quotes an example.
3. `explicit_denial`: it explicitly denies receipt or understanding of the
   applicable notice, without an unresolved conflicting self-assertion.
4. `quoted_or_directive_only`: apparent acknowledgment occurs only in quotes,
   examples, attributed speech or classification instructions.
5. `notice_discussion`: it discusses the notice without such self-assertion,
   denial or apparent quoted acknowledgment.
6. `none`: none of those relations is expressed.

The initial N3 quote belongs to the quoted category under this proposal. N5's
refusal to read or acknowledge is not automatically a denial of prior receipt;
its actual text and temporal scope need independent labeling. N7 needs
externally stipulated role/attribution facts; its own claim to speak for the
receiver cannot determine those facts. Add mixed genuine assertion/quotation,
explicit denial, hold, and acknowledgment-with-hold contrasts.

Disposition is separate. For each applicable obligation, propose `refused`,
`held`, `ordinary_response` (neither stated), or `unresolved` (materially
incompatible or ambiguous dispositions under the same scope and time).
Different obligations can have different dispositions; retain that vector
rather than forcing partial acceptance into a catch-all `other`. A clearly
resolved temporal change is not unresolved conflict. Neither stated
acceptance nor disposition proves authority or action.

Adequacy needs obligation-level rationale: addressed, missing, or
indeterminate. Proposed complete means all semantic obligations addressed;
partial means substantive requested content is supplied while some obligation
is missing or indeterminate. Retain that uncertainty separately. Off-target
means substantive content addresses none of the requested obligations.
Claim-only applies to a completion/promise claim without a substantive answer;
no-answer supplies neither. Unresolved applies when complete context cannot
determine the relevant relation. Mixed irrelevant prose does not erase useful
partial content, and a refusal does not erase returned content.

Refine W6 before freezing a key: an ambiguous reference may leave one
obligation indeterminate while the requested explanation is plainly missing.
Add actual refusal-plus-partial-output and partial-plus-off-target examples.
Keep sentence count, no-edit observation and arithmetic outside semantic
adequacy. Choose a genuinely semantic obligation before testing whether a
semantic hint adds anything to deterministic checks.

Eligibility is question-specific. Missing output can block adequacy while a
separately admitted acknowledgment remains inspectable. Unavailable context,
truncation, wrong-target admission and unsupported state are no-call outcomes;
complete ambiguous evidence can receive semantic unresolved. Neither outcome
changes earlier proved stages.

## Selected-source assertions

Keep supported, contradicted, unsupported extension and unresolved distinct.
Missing warrant is not an incompatible fact. Scope each assertion to its
component, route, population, attempt, time and claimed observation:

- An explicit statement that a preparation does not establish a passive reader
  contradicts an assertion that **that preparation establishes one**. It does
  not prove that no reader exists anywhere.
- Constructor writes contradict the claim that **that inspected constructor
  is passive**. They do not rule out a separately implemented reader.
- A resolver that omits range enforcement contradicts the claim that **it
  enforces that range**. A claim that all accepted installed packages happen
  to satisfy the range instead needs an actual same-scope counterexample or
  remains unsupported. A synthetic counterexample must be marked invented.
- A failed terminal contradicts successful completion of that same attempt.
  Output availability and an observed caller-check pass remain separate.
- Refusal contradicts acceptance of the same request/obligation at the same
  relevant time; it need not negate receipt or earlier useful output.

Split queued-to-receipt and queued-to-fulfillment promotion into separate
assertions with the same whole evidence. Retain original compound specimens
as diagnostics. Relevant conflict requires incompatible records about the
same proposition with no applicable precedence already resolving them;
irrelevant disagreement must not defeat a warranted assertion. Preserve
resolved-versus-ambiguous matched referents and distinct unavailable-context
controls.

Choice and Noul decomposition remain alternatives. Decomposition needs a
complete component truth/abstention/inconsistency table, retained raw answers,
component labels, combination identity and full cost. Question errors are not
assumed statistically independent. No probability product, confidence threshold,
batching strategy, arm selection or prior twelve-case bar is adopted here.

## State projection and missing delivery

Build any later state projector from retained raw draft/source artifacts,
not rendered Markdown with line prefixes. Bind the raw bytes, selected draft
coordinates, assertion occurrence, encoding/normalization mapping, exact state
and question bytes, exclusions and source order. Mechanical readiness does
not prove unique assertion selection, adequate context or adequate sources.
Retain whole sources; over-limit or unestablished token fit remains no-call,
without silently excerpting evidence.

The existing producer admits committed-file packets. It does not capture
unsaved early drafts, select assertions or sources, qualify interpretation
state, or deliver hints into native caller work. Interpretation additionally
needs current intent/amendments, admitted request/response/events, role/quote
and referent selection, complete output assembly, correlation and intervention
records. These are missing producers, not installed equipment.

The dated primary documentation supplies Choice/Noul text-state and question
contracts. Question IDs are not model instructions; all meaning belongs in
the actual instructions/criteria. Confidence derives from the answer
probabilities and is not independent confirmation. Documentary token limits,
prices and rate limits supply neither token fit nor account/run entitlement.
A later runner must reconcile or disable default retries under its frozen
attempt policy. Source changes get graduated compatibility assessment.

## Labels, controls and measured usefulness

Independent labelers inspect complete permitted source/context/control
material without constructor recommendations. A separate adjudicator settles
relation, evidence, source-selection adequacy, missing information and allowed
consumer response. Freeze the withheld key before inference; keep labels,
rationales, pair membership and critical flags outside all inference arms.
Tuning/diagnostic cases need a separately declared evaluation boundary before
any calibration claim.

Preserve ordinary-model and deterministic baselines, matched legitimate cases,
omission controls, service/transport errors, invalid answers, model identity
uncertainty, semantic unresolved, abstention, acquisition no-call and unattempted
slots. The actual methods, arms, repetitions, ordering, acceptance requirements
and data/provider scope belong to the later reviewed packet and decision.

A supplied deliberate-error classification establishes no naturally occurring
mistake prevented. Native workflow value needs qualified first-judgment capture,
timely hint delivery, actual correction or rejection, preserved final quality
and downstream rework observations. Required independent review stays in all
arms. Replacement additionally needs evidence of work actually removed.

Use the maintained whole-workflow accounting contract. Reconcile case,
question, logical slot, request, attempt and combination identities for these
new cases; old 60/144-slot schemas do not transfer automatically. Measure
research/equipment, labels/review, acquisition/selection, source resolution,
projection/hash/rendering, limit checks, calls/retries/cache, consumption,
correction/fallback, required review, maintenance, interruptions/recovery,
grading, cleanup and handback. Count shared events once, distinguish setup,
recurring and evaluation work, and avoid summing overlapping elapsed spans.
Missing measurements stay unknown; API-rate equivalents are not bills or
subscription capacity. Cheap calls alone establish no saving.
