## Interpretation: correct the question and case boundaries

The interpretation report correctly separates receiver acknowledgment, stated disposition, and semantic answer adequacy from receipt, identity, consent, execution, retrieval, and literal caller checks (`591/source-initial/report.md:14-64`). Refusal can coexist with acknowledgment or useful returned content. Complete but ambiguous evidence differs from unavailable context. These distinctions should survive refinement.

Its proposed Choice categories are not yet mutually exclusive. `mention_only` covers discussion of an acknowledgment, while `quoted_spoof_only` covers quoted/example acknowledgment text; N3 fits both descriptions. N5 explicitly discusses refusing the notice, yet receives `none_other`, conflicting with the broad mention criterion. N7 combines a classification command with an assertion of speaking for the receiver; that text alone cannot establish impersonation or genuine origin. Speaker provenance belongs to deterministic admission. An acknowledgment question can classify a receiver's expressed assertion, not establish that the speaker really read or understood the notice.

A concrete correction is to give the acknowledgment relation this priority: unresolved self-assertion/referent conflict; otherwise explicit receiver self-assertion about the applicable notice; otherwise apparent acknowledgment confined to quotations, examples, attributed speech, or classification commands; otherwise notice discussion without self-assertion; otherwise none. Refusal stays a separate disposition. Apply the revised meaning to N3 and N5 through independent labeling. N7 needs externally stipulated attribution and quote boundaries; do not derive its spoof label from its own provenance claim. Add a mixed genuine-self-assertion-plus-quotation case to verify precedence, and distinguish explicit denial from explicit acknowledgment with refusal. This is a proposed definition, not an accepted answer key.

Disposition also needs a complete partition. `ordinary_response` currently means neither refusal nor hold, leaving `other` with no defined remainder. Define temporal resolution for "I refuse X but will do Y once Z" and partial acceptance; unresolved should mean genuinely incompatible or ambiguous disposition for the same applicable obligation. Either define a real remaining category or remove it before freeze. Add a held case and an ack-plus-held contrast, because N1-N6 currently do not exercise the held option.

Adequacy needs obligation-level rationale and a declared priority for mixed answers (`591/source-initial/report.md:56-102`). A partly relevant answer plus unrelated prose can satisfy both partial and off-target wording. A partial answer with a completion claim should retain partial adequacy; a refusal with substantive output can retain that output's adequacy. Define off-target as substantive content satisfying none of the requested semantic obligations; claim-only and no-answer then apply when no substantive requested answer exists. Reserve unresolved for uncertainty that prevents determining the obligation relation, not for clearly omitted requirements.

W6's "That acknowledgment proves it" does not automatically warrant unresolved for the entire two-part request: the required explanation may be plainly missing even while its referent is ambiguous. Independent labelers should identify which obligation is decidable, which is missing, and which is indeterminate. W1's two-sentence condition and no-edit condition belong to their declared caller checks; the semantic complete label does not establish either condition. Add the promised refusal-plus-partial-output case as actual text, and a mixed partial/off-target case, before claiming those contrasts are covered. The current twelve semantic examples are a useful starting inventory, not proof of complete category coverage.

Keep N8-N10 and W7 as explicit no-call controls with separate receipt/retrieval/admission observations. Specify question-level eligibility: a missing result can prevent adequacy assessment without erasing a separately available acknowledgment observation. Retain the exact sorting/summation control as mechanical work. No semantic call is justified merely because its JSON can be rendered as prose.

## Assertions: distinguish incompatibility from missing warrant

The assertion report's four-way Choice definitions and four Noul propositions preserve the needed relation distinctions (`592/source-initial/report.md:39-71`). Source-selection adequacy remains outside mechanical readiness. The literal baseline appropriately leaves paraphrase support undecided and treats quotation matching as a literal observation (`:89-101`).

Several proposed cases still need narrower assertions or stronger stipulated evidence:

- The passive-reader sanity specimen (`:33`) can be contradicted when its proposition is specifically that the preparation documents an established reader and the complete preparation explicitly states otherwise. It does not prove that no reader exists. State this documentary scope in the assertion and key. A statement that no reader has been established provides absent warrant for the broader existence claim.
- Active constructor writes (`:80`) contradict "this inspected constructor is passive." They do not exclude a separate independent reader using GET/parsing components. Narrow the challenged component, route, and time; preserve broader availability as unsupported or unexamined.
- Resolution without fallback-range enforcement (`:82`) contradicts an assertion that this resolver enforces that range on installed packages. It does not alone falsify the universal assertion that all accepted installed packages happen to satisfy the range. For that assertion, add a same-scope accepted out-of-range example or an explicit incompatible requirement fact.
- Failed terminal status (`:83`) contradicts successful terminal completion for the same attempt. A correct-looking returned result is not an observed caller-check pass. Conversely, terminal failure alone does not settle result adequacy. Separate terminal status, output availability, and caller validation in both challenged and legitimate assertions. The interpretation report already preserves these dimensions.
- A refusal contrast (`:79`) contradicts acceptance only when the request, obligation, time, and disposition are the same. Refusal can coexist with receipt or prior useful output; it is not a generic negation of receipt or fulfillment.

The queued/received-and-fulfilled assertion bundles stages. Split receipt promotion and fulfillment promotion into distinct assertions sharing the same complete evidence, so a label or error identifies the mistaken step. Do likewise for other conjunctions where one component is warranted and another is contradicted or unwarranted. Preserve the original compound specimens as diagnostic evidence rather than deleting them.

Relevant conflict requires incompatible records about the same proposition, attempt, scope, and time, with no source-precedence rule already resolving them. Independent provenance in an invented source is a construction stipulation, not observed independence. The legitimate account-existence control is useful: disagreement about terminal success need not defeat the existence of two accounts. Ambiguous referents should be varied against the same resolved control so ambiguity, missing source context, and service uncertainty remain distinct.

For Noul decomposition, freeze a total truth/abstention table and labels for every component as well as the final relation. Referent criteria must cover missing or underdetermined subjects, not only multiple named readings. A simultaneous warrant/incompatibility answer needs retained component evidence and an inconsistency outcome; it must not disappear inside a plausible unresolved aggregate. Documented independent question evaluation does not establish statistically independent errors. Do not multiply probabilities as if independent, infer completeness from a Noul value, or transfer Choice confidence thresholds between changed taxonomies. Bands, packaging, and decomposition remain candidates.
