## Producer contracts and observation boundaries

### Claim support

The claim producer requires version 1 case data, a nonempty assertion, a committed regular-file draft with enclosing and assertion line coordinates, and one or two committed regular-file sources with expected SHA-256 digests. It can retain missing, changed, unsupported, controlled-unavailable, or malformed-context outcomes. Ready output includes packet JSON, rendered Markdown, complete acquired source artifacts, and a receipt with producer/input/artifact identities, byte counts, and preparation duration. Literal quotation status is in JSON; the Markdown does not display that field. [Claim source][claim-producer].

The rendered packet is the supported semantic input. It contains a supplied assertion, ordinary enclosing context, and complete ordered sources; it excludes evaluator labels. It does not find the assertion, resolve citations, or prove that the selected sources exhaust its basis. Its committed-draft reader cannot directly consume an unsaved working draft. These limits explain why preparation timing and selection effort remain consequential. [Workflow investigation][workflow]; [claim contract][claim-contract].

The existing question has four relations: supported, contradicted, unsupported_extension, and unresolved. An unestablished event is not thereby contradicted; a derivative claim is not independent corroboration. Non-ready acquisition is a no-call fallback, not the semantic unresolved category. The corpus lacked a genuine conflicting-source control. [Contract][claim-contract].

### Publication evidence delivery

Coverage input supplies parent and child commit identities, ordered source references, indexes selecting the verification and review records, and optional overlay bytes. The parent must be a direct parent of the child. Overlays produce a derived snapshot, not a historical commit. The producer records candidate relationships as equal, different, absent, or unavailable, separately checks the review digest, and supplies complete candidate/source artifacts plus a receipt and table. Unsupported deletions/entry kinds and acquisition errors remain explicit. [Coverage source][coverage-producer].

This is a useful independently usable deterministic component for publication preparation. Its current table does not deliver changed-text interpretation or establish which review obligations apply. Missing map membership is neither proof of a defect nor exemption from review. A direct-parent candidate is not the observed pushed range. Consumer selection of the applicable range and records remains work to account for. [Workflow investigation][workflow]; [coverage results][coverage-results].

It prepares metadata without starting a task; execution separately creates a thread/turn and steers one amendment after the first qualifying implementation change. RPC acceptance establishes server acceptance, not comprehension. Tool inventories and final capture cover regular files with declared exclusions at non-atomic read boundaries. Child histories and complete child usage are not collected by implication. [Collector source][collector]; [observation contract][observation-contract].

## Independent labels, controls, and smallest prerequisites

### Labels and context

Independent evaluators should label the actual permitted evidence before model testing, with expected outcomes and corrections held outside candidate input. Freeze exact question meanings, assertions/requests, source roles/order, acquisition states, candidate identities, and grading dimensions. Keep factual mechanism labels, semantic adequacy, safety, task alignment, collection completeness, and final quality separate. A label dispute needs adjudication and a newly bound key, not silent relabeling.

For receipts and continuation, derive identity/correlation and native-ID equality from the records deterministically. Evaluate acknowledgment meaning and textual fulfillment independently against the declared request. Preserve exact original consent, amendments, and withdrawals when they affect the consumer; a peer-labelled user-role submission does not authenticate operator origin. Inaccessible or truncated context cannot be repaired by an invented approval summary. These requirements reuse the [comparison method][comparison-method] and [operation contract][operation-contract].

### Controls

Preserve the existing comparison priorities: unnecessary interruptions first, then constrained expensive-model capacity; report latency and money separately. A new reliability threshold or quality/cost trade-off remains the owning decision's choice. [Workflow investigation][workflow].

Every selected use needs:

1. The current ordinary caller/author behavior with required review and actual checks.
2. Deterministic preparation/checking on its declared supported inputs.
3. Omission of the semantic addition, with ordinary work still measured.
4. Any proposed semantic arm supplied the same relevant context before first judgment.

Keep unchanged/accurate claims, explicit contradictions, unsupported scope extensions, genuine independent source conflicts, missing referents, stale/wrong-target records, receipt without ACK, ACK without completed work, failed acquisition, refused/held inputs, replacement IDs, and lost-response uncertainty visible. Construction labels test invented handling; they do not establish live fault production or recovery.

## Access, model, transfer, and host gates

Before any dependent action, the owning packet must establish:

- **Source/candidate:** exact producer, route, API/adapter/runtime, prompt, context, review, and evaluator identities; changed dependencies invalidate affected evidence.
- **Caller and target:** actual callable interface, supplied native task or explicit creation contract, current owner/server/store/session, wrapper/native/turn/message chain, independent reader, and return path where required.
- **Access and authority:** applicable task/purpose consent, private reads, authentication, credential scope, provider/data transfer, creation/send/setup/cleanup permissions, and verified original grants. A semantic score supplies none of these.
- **Model/route:** actual supported requested and returned model, effort where observable, endpoint/billing route, quota/resource admission, and bounded requests. Current public Jev aliases do not pin behavior; SDK automatic retries must not override the accepted no-retry/reconciliation policy.
- **Host/observer:** approved namespace, writable runtime paths, profile/configuration/resources, network reachability, idle/busy condition, event coverage, source correspondence limits, and owner coexistence. A newly spawned SDK server is not evidence about an independently running GUI owner.
- **Execution and restoration:** reviewed exact inputs and stop/reconciliation/cleanup contract, fresh fixture bindings, operation-specific grant, retained outcomes and restoration, then independent result review.

The accepted fixture/probe sequence keeps method choice, setup preparation, setup authorization, acquired fixtures, exact operation grant, eight profile probes, restoration, result review, and integration distinct. Source support does not propagate a live or provider grant. [Cohort contract][cohort-contract]; [method choice][method-choice].

TypeSafe documentation currently declares SDK retries by default and a response model identifying the versioned service model. That is a documentary reason to bind retry and model policy explicitly, not evidence that any account can execute this study. The current public model page declares a 64k-token total request budget and a 32k-token state-plus-longest-question budget. The retained comparison also bound serialized bytes; token fit cannot be inferred from a byte cap. Oversized sources need an explicit unsupported outcome rather than silent truncation. Choice confidence is a function of option probabilities; it is not a second correctness observation. No cookbook threshold is calibrated for these portfolio questions. [Models][typesafe-models]; [confidence][typesafe-confidence].

## What the consumer can conclude

A **correlated receiver receipt** can establish portfolio notification; explicit **ACK** is a separate claim. A **caller-checked result** can establish bounded work fulfillment; a worker completion claim alone cannot. **Same-task continuation** needs the profile's identity and prior-context evidence; a replacement or fork remains separately reported. **Source correspondence** describes captured bytes at recorded boundaries; it does not identify current runtime state or prove continuous stability. **Independent checking** may catch errors earlier even when the author has already reasoned about them; **reasoning replacement** additionally needs evidence that work disappears. These are separate accepted claims and hypotheses, not interchangeable score interpretations. [Operation contract][operation-contract]; [observation contract][observation-contract]; [joined investigation][joined].

I propose no new maintained procedure. The existing comparison contract already requires adequate first-judgment context, omission and deterministic controls, complete accounting, and bounded conclusions. Useful consumer corrections are concrete: preserve the join's distinction between reasoning replacement and added checking; deliver changed texts and separate review association rather than another relationship summary; identify the actual corrected collector revision and writable runtime boundary; and make the supported artifact format explicit when a producer field is metadata-only. These belong with their existing owners after evidence and review, not in a second procedure.

The method-choice handback should record each selected or deferred use, its maintained revision, invocation condition, required result, and exact downstream consumer. The reviewed catalog-update procedure was not supplied or independently established here; dependent reconciliation remains with its producer, without blocking this assessment or ordinary operation controls. [Research consumer contract][research-task]; [method choice][method-choice].

This source assessment is complete with the stated evidence concerns. It neither accepts nor dismisses an optional semantic use. The next decision is which finite use, if any, warrants its explicit prototype/calibration prerequisites and later execution proposal. Independent final review, byte-preserving publication, catalog reconciliation, and any cohort exchange belong to the coordinator.
