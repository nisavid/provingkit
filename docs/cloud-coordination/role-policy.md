# Cloud logical-role policy

This policy retains accepted duties and six correspondence edges in the
current-tree fallback. It grants no new authority or account role and does not
establish permission enforcement. Native
children remain leader-owned children even when performing logical peer duties.

## Duties and ownership

**Foreman** is the operator-owned strategic role for priorities, policy,
judgment, and consequential exceptions within delegated authority. Delegate
routine routing and settled-rule execution to its Dispatch. Existing project
orchestrators retain delivery responsibility.

**Dispatch** is logically owned by its Foreman. Handle routine routing,
correlated acknowledgements and results, already-authorized rules, and accepted
continuation obligations. Refer ambiguity, missing authority, and consequential
exceptions to its own Foreman. Project implementation remains with its owner.

**Concierge** is the operator's front desk, performed by the current top-level
thread. Lightly classify requests, find work and owners, maintain a contact
directory, and retain responsibility until the recipient accepts the handoff.
Deep formulation belongs to an actual scoped Reception; delivery belongs to
the accepting project owner. Create no placeholder intake chats.

Start with one agent. Instantiate other roles only when useful and authorized;
three permanent peers are not a prerequisite. Record native identity, creation
ancestry, logical ownership, and project ownership separately. Titles and
directory entries transfer none of them.

## Permitted correspondence

| Sender | Recipient | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foreman | Its Dispatch | Foreman may reach Dispatch; routine routing normally uses its own. |
| Dispatch | Its Foreman | Own-Foreman permission, bound to accepted logical ownership. |
| Concierge | Foreman | Explicit Concierge exception to the protected Foreman inbox. |
| Foreman | Concierge | Every chat may reach Concierge. |
| Concierge | Dispatch | Concierge may correspond with every chat through supported controls. |
| Dispatch | Concierge | Every chat may reach Concierge. |

These six edges do not establish general all-to-all permission. A foreign
Dispatch routes through the receiving Foreman's Dispatch. Concierge's
exception does not transfer to Dispatch. Use an ownership chain where it adds
useful context. A separate bounded setup/test grant does not rewrite inbox
policy or extend itself to unrelated work.

Correspondence grants no additional project execution, spending, admission,
account change, hold release, or ownership transfer. Preserve the original
grant's target, actions, conditions, amendments, and holds. Reuse verified
applicable authority across handoffs; a copied policy is not proof of a grant.

## Selection and continuity

Requested child settings are Foreman `gpt-6-astra` / `ultra` and Dispatch
`gpt-6.1-terra` / `low`, subject to exact-surface capability and Rolecasting policy. Obtain a
disposition before activating an incompatible configuration; ordinary work
can continue through the simpler profile. Ordinary workers and independent reviewers use current
Rolecasting selection rather than these role defaults.

Concierge retains the current root's runtime settings. Record them when
exposed; prompts and child configuration cannot turn that root into Terra.
Requested settings remain separate from served-model evidence.

Bind each assignment to its owner, target generation, immutable inputs, and
explicit reply recipient. Native finals go to the native parent; sibling-owned
work requires a correlated reply and verified owner acceptance. Preserve late
results and uncertain attempts. Opening a store transfers no ownership.
New roots require explicit checkpoint handoff and verified bindings; old
handles alone do not establish continuity. Broader automatic startup and
shared-account rendezvous remain separately qualified extensions.
