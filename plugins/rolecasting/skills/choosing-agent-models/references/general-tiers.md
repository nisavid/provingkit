# General Model Tiers

Use these tiers only for work outside the three security classes after checking complete-operation Jev suitability. Select the lowest-cost proven pair preserving the assignment's judgment margin. Assess worker and orchestrator needs separately, including implementers, reviewers, writers, and integrators.

GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5 handle ordinary demanding work. Start precise, recoverable implementation, debugging, analysis, and focused tests on a supported Sol `medium` pair when its judgment margin suffices; raise effort with task demands. Default an independent reviewer to Sol `high` when the review is consequential but lacks the exceptional high-stakes cognitive conjunction below. Prefer Claude for UI, UX, and visual design, and GPT for review. Before dispatch, when an eligible and authorized other-provider target would satisfy the preference, recommend target reconsideration to the owning delegating workflow and wait for its revised plan before selecting on that target. Do not silently change the settled worker target. An explicitly operator-fixed target or the sole eligible target remains in force; choose its proven pair and disclose the preference limit.

| Effort | Required judgment |
| --- | --- |
| `ultra` / `ultracode` | Highly parallelizable orchestration that benefits from this effort; use the form the exact target accepts. Encourage applicable adjudicated or reconciled collaborative or competitive panels, or similar workflows, with each participant selected under its own effort policy. |
| `max` | For OpenAI and Anthropic models, every gate in [effort eligibility](effort-policy.md#max-effort), including the frontier problem conjunction and adjudicated or reconciled loop/panel workflow; other providers retain their own applicable rules. |
| `xhigh` | Hard or complex, substantial unknowns, hard to reverse, or expensive mistakes; any one condition suffices. |
| `high` | Somewhat hard or complex work that is relatively well understood; the normal consequential-review starting point. |
| `medium` | Some complexity with well-understood behavior. |

Choose from the target's supported efforts, rather than assuming every model exposes the table. Apply [effort eligibility](effort-policy.md) to all tiers; Opus is eligible for `max` only under its full conjunction and exact target support. Availability alone does not justify a higher effort. Ultra/Ultracode does not require max-effort participants or the frontier problem qualification. Use it when parallel orchestration materially helps; a small serial task or a target without the exact effort support stays on another eligible effort.

Use Claude Sonnet 5.5 for some-complexity work that exceeds Haiku or Luna's role, remains resolvable with shallow cognition, and is not sensitive to outcome quality. Route deeper reasoning or quality-sensitive outcomes to a suitable stronger model, ordinarily Sol or Opus. This middle tier is akin to a hypothetical GPT-6 Terra; no GPT-6 Terra is currently available, so never synthesize or advertise that route.

[Anthropic's model overview](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/overview) identifies Sonnet 5.5 as `claude-sonnet-5-5`; its [effort documentation](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/effort) lists API `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, and `max` support (checked 2026-09-30). These are provider facts, not proof that a particular executor accepts a pair. Select only its fresh catalog/schema intersection and apply the named-successor rule. Sonnet is outside the genius/workhorse medium floor but, as an Anthropic model outside the eligible top tier, is ineligible for max under this policy.

For moderately complex, well-understood work within their roles and without sensitivity to outcome quality, consider GPT-6 Luna `xhigh` or Grok 4.7 `high`. For simple, well-understood work without sensitivity to outcome quality, consider Haiku, GPT-6 Luna, or Grok at the lowest supported adequate effort; occasional low effort may fit. These alternatives have no fixed preference order. Prove role suitability, judgment margin, and the exact pair; if complexity exceeds Haiku/Luna, use the Sonnet middle tier when its shallow-cognition and quality conditions hold.

Reserve GPT-6 Astra or Claude Fable 5.1 for work that is both **high-stakes** and demands at least one of deep cognition, intuitive hypothesis, analysis or synthesis, thoughtful articulation, or judgment over substantial unknown unknowns. Complexity or a named cognitive need without high stakes stays on Sol or Opus. Astra is the more economical exceptional option; use it for a bounded assignment that preserves the high-stakes judgment, not to enlarge scope. Use Fable for choicest beneficial deep work only with a current successful [target proof](capability-probes-and-fallbacks.md#prove-fable). This general-tier conjunction does not replace the more specific Fable security fallback rule.
