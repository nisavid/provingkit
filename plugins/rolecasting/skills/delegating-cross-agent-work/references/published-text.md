# Delegating published text

Retain authority for architecture, root cause, what the text must say, and external actions. Choose the writer under the [published-text rule in choosing-agent-models](../../choosing-agent-models/references/general-tiers.md#published-text).

A coordinator assigns, tracks, and verifies the work. Workers do the work, including writing and revising published text, whatever model the coordinator runs on. Give the writer the facts the text must convey and check the returned wording against those facts.

Outside a coordinating role, keep writing local only when you qualify as a writer for that kind of text. Otherwise assign a qualified writer. Applying a qualified writer's text word for word is not writing. Any model may do so with the required authority.
