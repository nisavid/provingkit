# Raw scenario

A scarce release-trust specialist is available for one short decision window.
The coordinator has gathered these named artifacts:

- A: candidate bytes for the evidence seam;
- B: the governing contract's relevant acceptance clauses;
- C: the applicable authority record;
- D: a passing check for this seam;
- E: a failed check identifying a counterexample in this seam;
- F: the full source of an unrelated plugin;
- G: historical logs from another release whose inputs differ; and
- H: planning notes for an unrelated UI redesign.

A through E are necessary to decide sufficiency, including the adverse evidence
in E. F through H have no bearing on the seam, its contract, or its authority.
The gathered material is not yet a frozen specialist packet.
The specialist must decide whether one evidence seam is sufficient. After the
window, the coordinator can wait on checks, update status, integrate an
authorized result, publish through the owning workflow if separately allowed,
and maintain the tracker. Define the handoff, the specialist's exact return,
what the coordinator may relay, and what happens if new evidence changes the
decision record. Identify which artifacts belong in the frozen packet and which
remain with the coordinator.
